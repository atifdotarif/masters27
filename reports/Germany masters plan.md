# Germany master's plan (October 2027 start)

**Prepared:** 18 Oct 2026, for Atif (BS CS, FAST-NUCES Karachi, CGPA 3.41/4, AI Software Engineer I at Imperium Dynamics)
**Constraints used:** PKR 35–40 lakh blocked account + PKR 10–15 lakh other expenses · no IELTS (use the FAST English-medium "MOI" letter) · no GRE · tuition-free public universities · English only · goal: work in Germany for a few years, then return to Pakistan.
**Exchange rate used:** €1 = PKR 320.

> **How to read this:** facts marked ✅ were checked on official university or government pages on 18 Oct 2026. ⚠️ means the fact is unverified or based on last year's pattern. Almost no university has published its 2027 dates yet, so **re-check every deadline in January 2027.**

---

## 1. The short version

- **Germany fits your budget.** The blocked account is €11,904 now ✅. Expect about **€12,600–13,000 (≈ PKR 40–42 lakh)** in 2027 ⚠️, because the amount is tied to the German student-grant rate (BAföG), which rises in 2027. The other one-time costs come to about **PKR 9–13 lakh**.
- **About 10 good tuition-free programmes accept your MOI letter with no GRE.** Most famous universities (RWTH, TU Berlin, Saarland, Bonn, TU Hamburg) require IELTS/TOEFL or the GRE, so they're out.
- **The real risk is the visa queue, not admission.** Apply early, choose programmes with early deadlines, and keep the April 2028 (summer semester) start as a fallback.
- **Grades are fine.** 3.41/4 converts to roughly **German 1.6–1.9** (modified Bavarian formula; each university does its own conversion) ⚠️. That clears every grade cutoff on this list.

## 2. Money

### Blocked account (Sperrkonto)
| | Amount | PKR |
|---|---|---|
| Current requirement (since 1 Jan 2025) ✅ | €11,904/yr (€992/month) | ≈ 38.1 lakh |
| Likely 2027 requirement ⚠️ | ≈ €12,600–13,000/yr | ≈ 40–42 lakh |

This is **your own living money**, paid back to you monthly in Germany. It's not a fee. Check the amount on [pakistan.diplo.de](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/1995528-1995528) in spring 2027 before you fund the account. If it ends up above PKR 40 lakh, top it up from your expenses budget.

| Item | PKR lakh |
|---|---|
| HEC attestation + MOFA apostille + translations | ~0.3–0.5 |
| uni-assist (€75 first + €30 each extra ✅), ~6 applications | ~0.7 |
| Other application fees (e.g. RPTU €60) | ~0–0.3 |
| Blocked account setup, visa fee (€75 ✅), travel insurance | ~1.0 |
| Flight Karachi → Germany | 1.5–2.5 |
| First semester fee (€127–500, usually includes a Germany-wide transport ticket) | 0.4–1.6 |
| Rent deposit (2–3 months' cold rent) | 3.0–4.8 |
| Settling in (SIM, bedding, first weeks) | 1.0–1.6 |
| **Total** | **≈ 9–13 lakh** ✅ fits 10–15 lakh |

### Monthly reality in year 1
€992/month must cover rent (€350–700), health insurance (~€140), food (~€250) and phone/transport (~€60). That's **tight in Munich, Frankfurt and Hamburg**, and workable in Dortmund, Passau, Würzburg, Göttingen, Hildesheim and Chemnitz.

### Year 2
The blocked account covers only year 1. Pay for year 2 with a **working-student job** (Werkstudent; 20 hrs/week × €15–20 ≈ €1,200–1,500/month). Keep about **PKR 10 lakh untouched as an emergency buffer.**

### If you change your mind and take one PTE Academic test
PTE is computer-based, results come in about 2 days, and it costs about PKR 35–45k. It's not IELTS. It would add:
- **TU Dresden Computational Modeling & Simulation** (PTE 60, no GRE)
- **Bonn-Rhein-Sieg Autonomous Systems** (PTE 71; window **15 Nov 2026 – 15 Feb 2027**)
- **TU Dresden CS** (PTE 78)
- **Uni Bonn CS** (PTE 76, very selective)
- **OVGU Magdeburg** (PTE 76)

It also protects you if the embassy asks for a test certificate at the visa stage ⚠️ (some universities warn this can happen).

## 5. Process, step by step

### Step 1 – Documents (now → December 2026)
- [ ] **Degree + transcripts:** HEC attestation (eDAS, Rs 3,000/document), then MOFA apostille (Rs 3,000/document, online registration required).
- [ ] **MOI letter from the FAST registrar.** Ask for this exact wording: *"The medium of instruction and examination for the entire BS Computer Science programme was exclusively (100%) English."* Signed and stamped. Göttingen, Passau, Hildesheim and HPI all check for "sole/exclusive/100%".
- [ ] **FAST course descriptions** (module handbook) for all courses, ideally stating English as the language. Needed for Frankfurt UAS and the TU Darmstadt equivalence table.
- [ ] **CV** (tabular, 1–2 pages), **motivation letter** (one master version, adapted per programme), **2 references** (a FAST professor + your Imperium manager).
- [ ] **Work experience letters** from Imperium and SharkStack (dates, role, technologies). These count at HPI, Frankfurt and Hildesheim.
- [ ] Passport valid until at least 2029.

### Step 2 – Applications (January → July 2027)
- **uni-assist** ([uni-assist.de](https://www.uni-assist.de/en/)) checks foreign documents for many universities. Allow **4–6 weeks**. There are two kinds:
  - **Full application:** uni-assist forwards your application to the university (Dortmund, Passau, Frankfurt UAS).
  - **VPD:** a pre-check document you upload to the university's own portal (HPI/Potsdam, Würzburg, THI).
- **Direct portals:** TU Darmstadt (TUCaN), Göttingen, Hildesheim, Chemnitz (EduApplication).
- **APS is not required for Pakistan** ✅ (only China, India, Vietnam, Mongolia). Ignore blogs that say otherwise.

### Step 3 – Admission → money (the week an offer arrives)
- Accept the best offer.
- Open the blocked account online (Expatrio, Fintiba or Coracle; about 1–2 weeks). Only HEC scholarships count instead of a blocked account ✅.
- Buy travel health insurance for the visa. Get German public student insurance (TK, AOK etc., ~€140/month) at enrolment.

### Step 4 – Student visa
- As a **Sindh resident**, apply to the **German Consulate General Karachi** ✅ (Islamabad handles Punjab, KP, ICT, GB and AJK).
- Apply online at the **Consular Services Portal** ([digital.diplo.de/studium](https://digital.diplo.de/studium)) ✅. Upload your degree, transcripts and admission letter **from the start**, or the application stalls.
- Applications are handled **in the order received** ✅. There's no official wait time; reports range from weeks to over 12 months ⚠️. So **submit the moment you have any admission letter.**
- Typical documents: admission letter, blocked account confirmation, insurance, attested degree and transcripts, MOI letter, CV, motivation letter, photos, €75 fee.
- **If the visa is late:** ask the university for admission to the **summer semester (April 2028)** and send the new letter to the consulate. The embassy itself suggests this ✅.

### Step 5 – Housing (as soon as you're admitted)
- Apply to the **Studierendenwerk** student dorms in your city immediately (€300–450/month; long waitlists).
- Backup: WG-Gesucht flat-shares. **Never pay a deposit before a viewing or contract** (common scam).

### Step 6 – Arrival (September–October 2027)
- Register your address (**Anmeldung**) within 2 weeks.
- Enrol and pay the semester fee.
- Apply for your **residence permit** at the foreigners' office (Ausländerbehörde) before your visa expires.
- Start applying for Werkstudent and HiWi jobs right away.

## 8. Working in Germany

### During your studies ✅
- Limit: **140 full days or 280 half days per year.** University HiWi (student assistant) jobs don't count toward it.
- **Employee jobs only.** No freelance or self-employed work (e.g. delivery-app gigs) on a student visa.
- Minimum wage: **€13.90/hr (2026), €14.60/hr (2027).** Mini-job limit €603/month (2026), €633 (2027).

| Path | Pay | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Working student (Werkstudent) in AI/software | €15–22/hr ⚠️ | Best: covers living costs and often leads to a full-time offer |
| HiWi (student assistant) in a university AI lab | €13–15/hr | Easiest first job; usually English; good references |
| Odd jobs (warehouse, kitchen, retail) | Minimum wage | Safety net only |

### After graduating ⚠️
- **18-month job-search permit** after the degree.
- **EU Blue Card:** ~€45,934 salary threshold for recent graduates and shortage jobs (IT is one), €50,700 general (2026 figures).
- Entry-level AI engineer pay is roughly €50–60k. Modelled savings are **~€10–15k/year at entry level and ~€18–22k at mid level** (more in a cheaper city).
- **English-only jobs** are concentrated in **Berlin, Munich, Hamburg and Frankfurt**. Learning German to A2/B1 during your master's roughly doubles your options. Passau and Fulda require A1 anyway.

## 9. Risks and fallbacks

| Risk | What to do |
|---|---|
| Visa appointment too slow | Apply to early-deadline programmes (Würzburg AI & XR, Frankfurt, Göttingen, Dortmund). Submit the visa application the day you get an offer. Switch to April 2028 if needed. |
| Blocked account rises above PKR 40 lakh | Top up from the expenses budget; check the amount in spring 2027 |
| Rupee weakens | Convert to euros early, once you hold an admission |
| MOI rejected by the embassy or a university | Take PTE (2-day results) as a quick backup |
| No admissions | Summer-semester windows: HPI (1 Dec), Passau AI Eng. (15 Dec), Hildesheim (15 Dec), Chemnitz/THI (15 Jan). Or work in the Gulf for a year and reapply for Fall 2028. |
| No Werkstudent job by year 2 | Pick a hub city; keep the PKR 10 lakh buffer; take a HiWi or part-time job meanwhile |
