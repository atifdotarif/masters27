## 1. Student visa refusal rates, processing times and 2025–26 restrictions by country

### Takeaway
Visa risk for Pakistanis rose sharply in 2025–26. The US F-1 refusal rate for Pakistan is reported at about 71%. The UK went from about 5% to 34–41% quarterly refusals, and several UK universities paused Pakistani recruitment. Schengen short-stay refusals are about 46%. Germany's main bottleneck is the Islamabad embassy appointment queue, which can reach about 10–12 months for standard master's applicants. Canada has removed the PAL requirement for master's students at public DLIs. Australia keeps Pakistan at Evidence Level 3. Türkiye and Korea are anecdotally easier, but no official data was found.

### Cited Findings
**UK**
- Pakistan's UK student visa issuance rate was 66% in Q2 2026 (a 34% refusal rate), against 95% in Q2 2025. — [British Council Opportunities Insight](https://opportunities-insight.britishcouncil.org/short-articles/news/decline-uk-student-visas-issued-first-half-of-2026)
- ICEF Monitor reports that Pakistan's UK study visa rejection rate rose nearly six-fold, from just under 6% to 41%, in the six months to March 2026. — [ICEF Monitor, May 2026](https://monitor.icef.com/2026/05/uk-sponsored-study-visa-issuances-down-rejection-rates-up-and-more/)
- A Wego analysis of Home Office data gives Pakistan 18.9% for the year to June 2026, up 13.9 points. The annual and quarterly figures differ by period and method. — [Wego](https://blog.wego.com/uk-student-visa-refusal-rate-how-hard-is-it-to-get-approved/); see also [Times Higher Education](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/uk-student-visa-refusal-rates-rise-amid-falling-applicant-numbers)
- The Basic Compliance Assessment visa-refusal threshold for sponsors fell from 10% to 5%. One source says the 5% limit applies to assessments from 1 June 2026. Universities reported as pausing or restricting Pakistani recruitment (mostly late 2025): Chester, Wolverhampton, East London, Sunderland, Coventry, Hertfordshire, Oxford Brookes, Glasgow Caledonian and BPP. — [The Daily Star (citing FT)](https://www.thedailystar.net/campus/education/news/several-uk-universities-close-door-bangladeshi-and-pakistani-students-ft); [Dunya Consultants](https://dunyaconsultants.com/uk-universities-restrict-pakistani-students/)
- Derby suspended recruitment from Pakistan and Bangladesh until at least Autumn 2026. — [The PIE News](https://thepienews.com/derby-suspends-pakistan-and-bangladesh-recruitment-as-uk-compliance-pressures-grow/)
- The Home Office has no nationwide ban on Pakistani students. The pauses are decisions by individual universities. — [Dunya Consultants](https://dunyaconsultants.com/uk-universities-restrict-pakistani-students/)

**US F-1**
- The overall F-1 refusal rate was 35% in 2025 (31% in 2024, 23% in 2015), a 10-year high. Asia is at 41%. Pakistan is at 71% and Bangladesh at 73%, and the trend "intensified over the past year". India rose from 53% to 61%. — [ICEF Monitor, 15 Apr 2026 (Shorelight analysis of State Dept data)](https://monitor.icef.com/2026/04/visa-rejections-climb-in-the-us-for-international-students-from-key-markets-including-india/)
- In January 2026 the US put an indefinite hold on processing for nationals of 39 travel-ban countries, but F, J and M processing continued. Pakistan was not named as a travel-ban country in the ICEF text. — [ICEF Monitor](https://monitor.icef.com/2026/04/visa-rejections-climb-in-the-us-for-international-students-from-key-markets-including-india/)
- F and M student applicants must set their social media profiles to "public". This follows the June 2025 State Department directive on reviewing applicants' online presence. — [Pakera](https://pakera.pk/us-visa-social-media-public-categories-pakistan/); [FAST Consultants](https://fastconsultants.pk/tips-for-getting-an-f-1-visa-for-pakistani-students-the-complete-2026-guide/)

- Student (F/M/J) appointment waits per a tracker relaying State Dept data (updated July 2026): Islamabad about 1 month, Karachi about 1.5 months. Peak season (June to August) can push slots weeks out. — [VisaGrader](https://visagrader.com/us-visa-appointment-wait-times/pakistan-pak); [GlobalGrads](https://www.globalgrads.org/f-1-student-visa-timeline-2026-real-costs-for-pakistani-students)
- Apply 3–6 months before the program starts. Expect about 2–4 months from I-20 to passport return. — [GlobalGrads](https://www.globalgrads.org/f-1-student-visa-timeline-2026-real-costs-for-pakistani-students)

**Germany**

- Türkiye student visas average about 15 days, or 2–6 weeks depending on the source. — [Eammu](https://eammu.com/travel-resources/visa-processing-time-tracker/turkey-national-visa-processing-time-for-pakistan); [Edify Elite](https://edifyelite.com.pk/blog/how-pakistani-students-can-apply-for-the-turkey-student-visa-in-2026)

### Inferences
- The UK is the highest-variance option. Refusals are up roughly six-fold, and lower-tariff universities, which a 3.41 CGPA budget applicant would often target, are the ones restricting Pakistanis. Any UK shortlist should be checked university by university, favouring higher-ranked sponsors with low refusal rates.
- For Germany, the appointment queue is a bigger constraint than the visa decision. The applicant's 3.41 CGPA falls below the reported 3.7 priority threshold. For a Fall 2027 start (admissions around Jan–Jul 2027), a standard 10–12-month wait could miss the intake. He should register on the Consular Services Portal waiting list as early as possible (the reported practice is to register before the admission letter arrives; verify this), or get into a priority category such as a DAAD scholarship.
- At a reported ~71% refusal rate, the US F-1 route carries the highest visa risk. Full funding and strong ties help, but the risk stays significant.
- With the PAL removed for master's, Canada's main barrier is now proof of funds: CAD 22,895 plus tuition, which is far above PKR 30 lakh unless funding covers it.

### Gaps
- No official 2026 Pakistan-specific refusal rates were found for Canada, Australia, Italy, the Netherlands, Ireland, Sweden, Finland, Hungary, Korea or Türkiye. National D-visa statistics are not published in the sources found.
- No current official Italy, Netherlands or Ireland appointment waits for Islamabad or Karachi were found. Italy's visa services in Pakistan are reported to be very congested, but I found no source to cite.
- The Pakistan figure in Australia's September 2026 Evidence Level table was not confirmed.
- The time period of the US 71% Pakistan figure is not stated in ICEF (likely 2025). Live US wait times should be checked on travel.state.gov.

## 2. English tests: IELTS vs PTE vs TOEFL vs Duolingo, and the MOI letter

### Takeaway
IELTS Academic (about PKR 60,000–72,000) is the safest single test because it is accepted everywhere, including by UKVI and most scholarships. PTE (about PKR 35,000–45,000) is cheaper and widely accepted in the UK, Australia and Canada. Duolingo (about USD 65–70, roughly PKR 19,000–20,000) is cheapest but is not accepted for the UK student visa and depends on the institution. An English-medium MOI letter can replace IELTS at many German universities and some Hungarian ones. I found no FAST-NUCES-specific confirmation.

### Cited Findings
- IELTS computer-delivered Academic costs about PKR 66,400–72,000 depending on city (one outlier source says about PKR 42,000). IELTS for UKVI costs PKR 60,500–72,000. Paper-based IELTS is reportedly no longer available in Pakistan. — [Premier English House](https://premierenglishhouse.com/ielts-fee-in-pakistan/); [Home Tuition Islamabad](https://hometuitionislamabad.com/ielts-fee-in-pakistan/); [Educrest](https://educrestmigration.com/how-to-book-ielts-exam-idp-vs-british-council-fees-dates-and-centres/)
- PTE Academic costs about PKR 35,000–45,000. — [Educrest](https://educrestmigration.com/ielts-vs-pte-vs-duolingo-english-test-pakistan/); [Gohata Global](https://gohataglobal.com/ielts-preparation-lahore.html)
- TOEFL iBT costs about PKR 38,000–45,000. It is widely accepted but used less from Pakistan. — [Educrest](https://educrestmigration.com/ielts-vs-pte-vs-duolingo-english-test-pakistan/)
- Duolingo costs about USD 70, roughly PKR 19,000–20,000. It is not accepted by UKVI for the UK Student visa, and acceptance varies by institution. — [AbroadIn](https://abroadin.pk/blog/duolingo-english-test-pakistan-2026/); [Educrest](https://educrestmigration.com/ielts-vs-pte-vs-duolingo-english-test-pakistan/)
- Comparison of which tests the UK, Canada and Australia accept. — [GlobalGrads score table](https://www.globalgrads.org/toefl-vs-pte-vs-duolingo-ielts-alternative-uk-canada-australia-2026-score-table)
- US universities that require TA applicants to show English proficiency: UGA requires international students to submit a qualifying English score with their TA application. — [UGA School of Computing](https://computing.uga.edu/graduate-financial-assistance)
- Germany: many universities accept an MOI certificate (an official letter from the Registrar or Controller of Examinations stating the degree was taught and examined in English) in place of IELTS. One commercial guide claims over 200 do. — [LeapScholar](https://leapscholar.com/blog/study-in-germany-without-ielts-universities/); see also [Expatrio](https://www.expatrio.com/about-germany/english-language-proficiency-test)
- Germany and Hungary accept MOI for admission and some government scholarships (low-reliability source). — [ScholarshipRegion](https://www.scholarshipregion.com/english-proficiency-certificate/)

### Inferences
- The best-value strategy is to take one IELTS Academic (target 7.0 overall with no band below 6.5, which covers most scholarships such as Chevening, Erasmus Mundus and DAAD EPOS, and Canadian and US programs). It costs about PKR 70,000. An MOI letter can supplement it for Germany, Hungary and Italy applications to save money where accepted. Results for computer-delivered IELTS and PTE typically arrive within days (general knowledge, not sourced here).
- If the US is a target, check whether each program accepts Duolingo. Many US CS departments still require TOEFL or IELTS for TA eligibility.

### Gaps
- No source confirmed whether FAST-NUCES issues an MOI or English Proficiency letter, or what it costs. Ask the FAST Karachi registrar or the Academic Office.
- No official 2026 PKR fees from the British Council, IDP, Pearson or ETS were captured, and the blogs conflict. Verify on the official booking pages.
- Result turnaround times were not captured from sources.
- Italy's MOI acceptance was not verified. Which scholarships (Chevening, DAAD, Erasmus Mundus, Stipendium Hungaricum, MEXT, GKS, Türkiye Burslari) accept MOI or Duolingo was not verified in this pass.

## 3. Document steps: HEC eDAS, MOFA, Apostille, CIMEA/DoV, WES, uni-assist, police certificate

### Takeaway
The standard order is HEC first, then MOFA attestation or Apostille, then the embassy or credential body. HEC moved to a fully online system on 11 May 2026 at Rs 3,000 per document, taking about 1–3 weeks. MOFA or Apostille costs about Rs 3,000 per educational document and takes about 3–10 working days (24–48 h urgent), and online registration is now required. CIMEA costs €150 and takes 60 working days (€100 extra for urgent), so it needs a very early start. WES (US) costs from USD 118 (document-by-document) or USD 186 (course-by-course).

### Cited Findings
- The new HEC Degree Attestation System took effect on 11 May 2026. It is fully online and charges Rs 3,000 per document, and an e-certificate is downloaded from the applicant's account. — [Nextup News](https://www.nextupnews.pk/2026/08/hec-degree-attestation-online-2026-fee.html); [Islamic Manpower](https://islamicmanpower.com/en/services/attestation/hec-degree)
- HEC timeline estimates conflict: 10–15 working days, 5–10 days, or "a few working days" online. — [Border n Bridges](https://bordernbridges.com/how-do-pakistani-students-get-their-documents-attested-for-studying-abroad-2026/); [UNIMENTOR](https://unimentor.study/hec-and-mofa-attestation-process-for-pakistani-students-2026-2027/14863/14/07/); [AbroadMate](https://abroadmate.me/guides/hec-attestation-mofa-complete-guide-2026)
- MOFA or Apostille costs Rs 3,000 per educational document (per the MOFA fee page as of 23 Aug 2026). Normal processing takes about 7–10 working days (another source says 3–5), and urgent processing 24–48 h. — [Pakera](https://pakera.pk/mofa-hec-database-mou-degree-attestation-apostille-october-2026/); [Hijrat](https://hijrat.pk/apostille-mofa-attestation-pakistan-2026.html); [Wego](https://blog.wego.com/mofa-attestation-pakistan/)
- From 27 July 2026, Apostille is not processed without prior online registration. The online appointment portal was reported temporarily suspended as a cybersecurity precaution. — [Hijrat](https://hijrat.pk/apostille-mofa-attestation-pakistan-2026.html)
- MOFA and HEC signed an MoU to link their degree databases (reported 12 Oct 2026). No start date or process change has been announced yet. — [Pakera](https://pakera.pk/mofa-hec-database-mou-degree-attestation-apostille-october-2026/)
- CIMEA Statement of Comparability (Italy) costs €150 (€122.95 + VAT) per qualification. Urgent processing costs €100 extra, and ordinary processing takes 60 working days. — [CIMEA official](https://cimea-diplome.it/page-comparability-service)
- WES US (2026, up 3% from 1 Jan 2026) Basic: document-by-document from USD 118, course-by-course from USD 186, ICAP USD 171/239, plus delivery fees. — [GradRight](https://gradright.com/what-is-wes-evaluation-how-much-are-wes-fees/); [StreamlinedAI](https://streamlinedai.app/blog/wes-credential-evaluation-guide)
- WES Canada: about CAD 329 standard and CAD 454 course-by-course. Budget CAD 40–80 extra for courier from Pakistan. — [IRCC.com](https://ircc.com/news/wes-canada-credential-evaluation-2026-step-by-step-guide)
- Pakistan-specific WES steps. — [Border n Bridges](https://bordernbridges.com/wes-credential-evaluation-for-pakistani-students-a-step-by-step-guide-2026/)
- German blocked-account documents: Islamabad requires the provider to give a verification contact, and the signature on the account papers needs a separate attestation (€25 cash) at a different appointment. — [Munaeem's Blog](https://www.munaeem.org/2026/02/german-embassy-islamabad-student-visa.html)

### Inferences
- Approximate total for the Pakistani-side attestation of a degree plus transcript: HEC 2 × Rs 3,000 and MOFA/Apostille 2 × Rs 3,000, about Rs 12,000 plus courier. Start right after the final degree is issued (2026 graduate). Allow 3–4 weeks, and more if the MOFA portal is suspended.
- Italy timeline: CIMEA's 60 working days (about 3 months) means applying by about Jan–Feb 2027 for Italian pre-enrolment and DSU scholarships. CIMEA can often replace the embassy's Declaration of Value, but this should be confirmed per university.
- WES is mainly needed for some US programs and for Canadian immigration. Most US and Canadian MS CS departments evaluate transcripts themselves, so WES should only be paid for where a program requires it.

### Gaps
- No 2026 uni-assist fee was found. It is widely known to be about €75 for the first application and €30 for each additional one, but this was not verified in this pass.
- No source was found for the Italian Embassy Islamabad Declaration of Value fee or timeline.
- No source was found for the cost or timeline of a Pakistani police character certificate (Sindh Police/Karachi; required for Canada, Australia and Korea), or of NADRA document attestation.
- No source was found for whether FAST-NUCES sends sealed transcripts or verifies directly for WES. The FAST verification fee was not found.

## 4. The funded MS route in the US and Canada

### Takeaway
Canada's thesis-based research MSc is the most realistic funded North American route. Several departments guarantee about CAD 20,000–25,000 per year to admitted thesis students who secure a supervisor (Guelph, Calgary, Alberta, UBC, Toronto). Under PKR 30 lakh, the applicant would still need to show proof of funds unless the funding letter covers it. Funded MS CS places in the US are rare (about 8–10% of international MS students get TA/RA). With a 3.41 CGPA, no publications and a ~71% F-1 refusal rate for Pakistanis, a funded US MS is a long shot. It is realistic only at mid-ranked public research universities, through RA positions arranged after cold-emailing faculty.

### Cited Findings
- UBC CS 2026–27: the international MSc package totals CAD 42,107. The faculty tuition award covers only year one, so net income from year two is CAD 24,728 (international). — [UBC CS Stipends 2026–27](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/grads/awards-support-current-grad-students/financial-assistantship/stipends-support-details-2026-2027)
- Guelph MSc CS guarantees at least CAD 20,000 per year for up to 2 years to international and domestic MSc students who secure an advisor and admission, through a GTA plus GRA mix. — [Guelph](https://www.uoguelph.ca/programs/msc-computer-science/)
- Calgary guarantees a minimum of CAD 24,000 per year for thesis MSc, for 2 years (older policy). — [UCalgary CSM Funding Policy](https://cumming.ucalgary.ca/sites/default/files/teams/13/@Funding%20Policy%20-%20CSM%20-%202025-FINAL.pdf)
- Alberta first-year MSc funding is about CAD 25,197 (older listing). — [MSc scholarships aggregator, Upgrad](https://www.upgrad.com/study-abroad/articles/scholarship-for-ms-in-canada/)
- U of Toronto CS: the enhanced package includes a take-home living allowance of CAD 39,500, and international MSc tuition is CAD 36,700.39 (2026–27). — [U of T CS Funding](https://web.cs.toronto.edu/graduate/funding-tuition-awards)
- CGS-M is CAD 17,500 for one year (eligibility for internationals is limited; check). — [ExpressTechJobs](https://www.expresstechjobs.com/computer-science-scholarships-in-canada/)
- In the US, about 10% of international MS students get TA/RA/GA funding, and about 8% in Computer and Information Sciences (commercial source, rough). — [GradPilot](https://gradpilot.com/news/ta-ra-ga-funding-reality-international-ms-students)
- UCSD: "MS students should not expect to be supported by either Research Assistantships or Teaching Assistantships." — [UCSD CSE](https://cse.ucsd.edu/graduate/financial-opportunities)
- Princeton's funded CS MSE is not accepting applications for Fall 2027. — [Princeton Grad School](https://gradschool.princeton.edu/financial-support/financial-support-model/master%E2%80%99s-degree-funding)
- UGA School of Computing runs a TA/RA application portal open to MS students (English score required for internationals). — [UGA](https://computing.uga.edu/graduate-financial-assistance). Note: the search snippet said the Spring 2027 portal "closed on 10/31/2026", which is after today's date (18 Oct 2026), so this detail is unreliable.
- Canadian visa funds: CAD 22,895 for living costs plus first-year tuition. — [IRCC.com](https://ircc.com/news/study-permit-financial-requirements-2026-cost-of-living-thresholds)
- US F-1: the I-20 requires proof of funds for the first year of costs (tuition plus living, minus any assistantship). At a reported 71% Pakistan refusal rate, funding letters and genuine-student evidence carry a lot of weight. — [ICEF Monitor](https://monitor.icef.com/2026/04/visa-rejections-climb-in-the-us-for-international-students-from-key-markets-including-india/)

### Inferences
- PKR 30 lakh is about CAD 14,000–15,000 or USD 10,700 at my assumed rates. That is below Canada's CAD 22,895 living-funds threshold alone. A fully funded thesis MSc, where the offer letter states the stipend and tuition coverage, is therefore effectively required. Canada counts funding letters as proof of funds (standard IRCC practice, not verified here).
- Strategy for Canada: identify 15–30 professors working in medical imaging/multimodal DL (matching his FYP) or LLM/agentic systems at Guelph, Calgary, Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Memorial, Dalhousie, Windsor, Concordia, UQAM/Laval-type programs, Regina, UNB and Ottawa. Cold-email them in Sept–Dec 2026 for Fall 2027. Most Canadian deadlines fall between Dec 2026 and Feb 2027. A 3.41 CGPA (about 83–85% equivalent) meets most minimums (often B+ or 3.0–3.3 in the last 2 years), but competitive programs (UBC, U of T, Waterloo) are reaches.
- US: a funded MS is not realistic as the primary plan on PKR 30 lakh. Combining a strong industry profile with a research-match RA at a mid-tier public university (e.g. smaller state R1/R2 schools) is possible but unpredictable, and the I-20 funding shortfall and visa refusal risk add to the difficulty. A US PhD (normally fully funded) may be a more realistic US entry point for a candidate with research output.
- GRE: most US MS CS programs went GRE-optional after 2020. Taking it (target 320+, Quant 165+) can offset a 3.41 CGPA at funding-oriented schools. This is general knowledge and was not verified for 2027 in this pass.

### Gaps
- GRE requirements for 2027 per university were not verified, and the GRE fee in Pakistan (about USD 220) was not captured from a source.
- No verified list of US universities that routinely fund MS CS students was found, and no Pakistan-specific Reddit success-rate data was found.
- Not verified: whether a Canadian university funding letter fully substitutes for the CAD 22,895 proof of funds in IRCC practice for Pakistani applicants.

## 5. Recommendation letters, SOP and quick profile strengthening

### Takeaway
His main assets are research-relevant industry experience (agentic AI, RAG, MCP, production systems), five Microsoft certifications, and a medical-imaging deep learning FYP. His main gaps are a moderate CGPA and no publications. The fastest boost in 3–9 months is to turn the FYP into a workshop or conference paper or an arXiv preprint, and to gather a supervisor letter and two academic letters.
