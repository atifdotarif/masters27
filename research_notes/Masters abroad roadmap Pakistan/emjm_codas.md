## Verdict for this applicant (read this first)

**Not viable as currently planned. Two separate problems each rule it out:**

1. **English: MOI is not accepted and neither is Duolingo.** [OFFICIAL] The site says: "No other methods of demonstrating English language skills than those listed below are accepted." It also says: "Duolingo and EF Set English language tests are not accepted." The English-medium-degree exemption covers only degrees from the EU/EEA, Australia, Canada, New Zealand, Singapore, South Africa, Switzerland, the UK or the US, earned while living there. The site adds: "An English-medium degree completed in any other country cannot be accepted." A FAST-NUCES MOI letter therefore does **not** count. **PTE Academic is accepted** (overall 62, writing 54), so PTE is the only workable path without IELTS. TOEFL is the other option.
2. **GPA: the Pakistan benchmark is 3.60/4.** [OFFICIAL] The site says: "the applicant is expected to have at least the following GPA: Bangladesh 3.70/4, India 8.0/10 or 80%, Pakistan 3.60/4." The applicant's CGPA is 3.41, below the benchmark. Relevance and academic performance are screened first, and only applicants who pass that screen are evaluated further. A 3.41 is very likely to be filtered out at the first stage, and 1.5–2 years of work experience does not offset the GPA bar in this screening model.

Recommendation: low priority. Apply only if the time and effort are spent anyway (there is no application fee on record, but the application does need a PTE score and a motivation video). Expect a rejection at the screening stage. If he applies, he could email codas@aalto.fi first to ask whether 3.41 is a hard cutoff.

## Autumn 2027 schedule ([OFFICIAL], admission.html)

The aggregator's dates are correct: **1 Dec 2026 – 5 Jan 2027**.
- Application form open: **1 Dec 2026, 09:00 to 5 Jan 2027, 15:00 (GMT+2)**. Late applications are not considered and the deadline "cannot be extended".
- Document upload deadline: **12 Jan 2027, 15:00 (GMT+2)**. Recommendation letters are also due by 12 Jan.
- Results: **end of March 2027**, by email.
- Accept offer: within 13 days for scholarship holders, within 7 days for reserve-list scholarships, and by 31 May 2027 for admitted students without a scholarship.

## Required documents ([OFFICIAL])
- Degree certificate and transcript as colour scans. The transcript must show the standard study period, total credits and grading scale.
- Official English translations if needed (not needed for FAST).
- Passport photo page.
- English test result: IELTS Academic, TOEFL iBT, PTE Academic or Cambridge C1/C2.
- **Motivation video**: at most 1 minute and 100 MB, provided as a link that must stay viewable until the end of March 2027.
- Motivation letter, typed in the online form, at most 500 words. It should cover why this programme, why you, and your preferred universities and why.
- CV.
- Links to course descriptions for relevant courses (FAST course outlines).
- Optional: at least one recommendation letter (an academic one is preferred), work certificates, publications.
- After admission only: certified copies, and proof of residence issued within the last 12 months.

## English requirement ([OFFICIAL])
Tests must be taken on or after 1 Dec 2024.
- IELTS Academic (including UKVI and Online): 6.5 overall, writing 6.0.
- TOEFL iBT taken before 21 Jan 2026: 92 overall, writing 22. TOEFL iBT taken on or after 21 Jan 2026 (new 1–6 scale): 4.5 overall, writing 4. Home Edition is OK; MyBest scores are not.
- **PTE Academic (including Online and UKVI): 62 overall, writing 54.** This is the route for this applicant.
- Cambridge C1 Advanced or C2 Proficiency.
- **Duolingo: NOT accepted. MOI letter: NOT accepted** (Pakistan is not an exempt country).

## Eligibility, selection, minimum GPA ([OFFICIAL])
- A bachelor's degree of at least 180 ECTS, or 3 years. A 4-year BS qualifies.
- Accepted fields: communications engineering, **computer science**, EE with a communications major, or software engineering.
- Expected knowledge (applicants should have "most of" these): linear algebra, calculus, probability, statistics, discrete maths; algorithms and data structures; software engineering and programming; operating systems; **basics of computer networks; basics of communications engineering; basics of mobile/wireless systems**.
- Partner-specific requirements: TU Braunschweig wants at least 60 ECTS in 8 of 10 CS core subjects and at least 15 ECTS of linear algebra, analysis and discrete maths. **UPC wants at least 5 ECTS each in wireless communications and in computer networks.** Grenoble (year 2) wants a strong computer-engineering background.
- Selection criteria, with no weights published: (1) relevance of previous studies, (2) academic performance, (3) recognition and quality of the institution, (4) suitability, (5) other competences. Criteria 1 and 2 form a first-stage screen. There is no minimum quota.
- Minimum GPA: no general minimum, but the benchmark for **Pakistan is 3.60/4**. Meeting it does not guarantee admission.
- Interview: none is mentioned; the motivation video takes its place. Técnico's separate double-degree route "may" use interviews, but that does not apply to the EMJM.

## Fit for a CS/AI-LLM background
- **CS is explicitly an eligible field** and no signal-processing prerequisite is stated. The TU Braunschweig degree is an M.Sc. Computer Science, and the Aalto, Braunschweig and Grenoble tracks include ML/data science.
- However, the expected knowledge includes communications-engineering basics and mobile/wireless systems. A typical FAST BS CS covers computer networks, OS, DSA, probability/statistics and linear algebra, but usually not communications engineering or wireless. He would meet "most of" the list, but **UPC as year-1 home university (wireless at 5 ECTS) is probably unavailable** to him.
- Year 1 is common to all students and includes a Communications block, so he should expect real telecom coursework even on an ML track. The programme leans towards communications/networking, with DS/ML as the second pillar. It is not an AI/LLM-focused master's.
- On fit alone (setting aside GPA and English) he would be a weak-to-moderate candidate. Aalto or TU Braunschweig with an ML focus would be the logical preference.
