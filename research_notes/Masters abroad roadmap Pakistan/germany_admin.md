# Germany admin facts for a Pakistani master's student (target intake: Winter Semester, October 2027)

Research date: 2026-10-18. The sources are official ones (pakistan.diplo.de, auswaertiges-amt.de, uni-assist.de, daad.de, bundesregierung.de, minijob-zentrale.de) unless marked otherwise.
Legend: **[VERIFIED]** means confirmed on an official page. **[PARTLY]** means official but incomplete or old. **[UNVERIFIED]** means I could not confirm it from an official source.

---

## 1. APS certificate: not required for Pakistan [VERIFIED by absence; no explicit official "no"]

- APS (Akademische Prüfstelle) exists only for **China, Vietnam, India and Mongolia**. DAAD and German embassy pages confirm the APS offices in Beijing, Hanoi and New Delhi (aps-india.de). None of these covers Pakistan.
- The Islamabad embassy's student visa page (dated 27.11.2025) and its financing page do **not mention APS anywhere**. The documents they require are degree certificates, transcripts and proof of tuition payment, uploaded via the portal.
- Some Pakistani consultancy blogs say APS is mandatory for Pakistan and give "aps-southasia.de" as the portal. **That domain does not exist**: a DNS lookup failed on 2026-10-18. Treat these claims as false.
- What Pakistani applicants need instead is the normal university/uni-assist document check (see section 2). For an HEC attestation of degrees, check each university's requirements; that point was not researched here.
- **Since APS does not apply, there is no APS fee, office or processing time to report.** Check again before applying in 2027, because the list of APS countries can change.

## 2. uni-assist fees [VERIFIED]

- **EUR 75** for the first chosen course of study in a semester.
- **EUR 30** for each additional course of study in the **same semester**.
- The same fees apply to VPD (preliminary review documentation). If you reapply in a new semester, you pay the full EUR 75/30 again. Fees are non-refundable, and some universities cover them.
- **Change in 2025–2026?** The official handling-fees page shows no fee change and no announcement for 2025, 2026 or 2027. These have been the rates for several years.
- Source: https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-apply/pay-all-fees/handling-fees/

## 3. Blocked account (Sperrkonto)

- **Current amount [VERIFIED]: EUR 992 per month = EUR 11,904 per year**, for visa applications from 1 January 2025. This is still the amount in force in 2026 (Pakistan mission page; 2026 checklists from the Hong Kong mission (April 2026) and the Dhaka mission (June 2026)).
  - In particular cases, the Islamabad embassy or Karachi consulate may require up to **2 years** of funds.
  - Signature attestation for the blocked account is done at the Islamabad embassy, the Karachi consulate or the honorary consul in Lahore. It costs **EUR 25 (about PKR 5,400), cash only**, and needs a separate appointment from the visa appointment.
  - Only **HEC** scholarships are accepted as proof of funds, not scholarships from Pakistani universities. A Verpflichtungserklärung (formal sponsor guarantee) from a sponsor living in Germany is also accepted.
- **Link to BAföG [VERIFIED in principle]:** The Federal Foreign Office says the amount is based on the "maximum rates for support received by German students", which means the BAföG maximum rate (currently 992 = 475 basic need + 380 housing + health/care insurance supplements).
- **Expected for 2027 [UNVERIFIED / projection]:**
  - Secondary sources (web.de, studierenplus.de) say the BAföG housing allowance will rise from **EUR 380 to EUR 440**. This was originally planned for WS 2026/27 and is now **postponed to the summer semester 2027 (1 April 2027)**. One source says the cabinet approved it on 12 Aug 2026. I could not confirm this on an official page.
  - If that happens and the visa rules follow, the requirement would be about **EUR 1,052 per month = about EUR 12,624 per year**. This is my own arithmetic, not an official figure.
  - The BAföG basic need is reported to rise from 475 to 503 for WS 2027/28. That would add another EUR 28 per month (about EUR 1,080 per month, roughly EUR 12,960 per year), also unconfirmed.
  - In 2024 the visa amount followed a BAföG increase only after a transition period (from 1 Sept 2024). The timing for 2027 is uncertain.
  - **Practical advice:** budget about **EUR 13,000** and check pakistan.diplo.de right before you fund the account (around spring/summer 2027).
- Sources: https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/1995528-1995528 ; https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/de/service/visa-und-aufenthalt/sperrkonto-388600

## 4. National student visa process in Pakistan (as of 2026)

### 4a. Consular Services Portal (Auslandsportal) [VERIFIED]
- Yes, student visa applications go through the **Consular Services Portal at https://digital.diplo.de/studium**.
- The steps are:
  1. Register.
  2. Fill in the interactive questionnaire, which generates your personal document list.
  3. Upload scans of your documents.
  4. A visa officer pre-screens them.
  5. You either get an appointment notification or are asked for corrected or additional documents.
- You can track the status online.
- Incomplete applications are processed more slowly.

### 4b. Islamabad vs Karachi [VERIFIED]
- **Embassy Islamabad** handles Islamabad Capital Territory, Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit-Baltistan and AJK.
- **Consulate General Karachi** handles **Sindh and Balochistan**. It uses the same portal, through a separate Karachi link on the embassy page. The consulate accepts only **permanent residents** of Sindh and Balochistan. Giving a false residence leads to refusal and possible prosecution.
- The Karachi PDF checklist found online (paper envelope drop-off, blocked account of EUR 10,236) is **outdated**. Do not rely on it; the portal procedure replaced it.

### 4c. Waiting list, appointments, priority
- **[VERIFIED, official page 27.11.2025]:**
  - Applications are processed **in the order received**, but demand exceeds capacity.
  - The consular section reports a "high volume" of student applications and asks applicants not to send inquiries.
  - The page does not mention priority categories.
  - It suggests submitting a **late-arrival acceptance letter** from the university, or an enrolment letter for the next semester, if your appointment comes too late.
- **Priority rules [PARTLY / historical]:** Dawn (28 Aug 2024) quoted embassy statements:
  - **May 2024 (summer semester):** scholarship holders and PhD students were prioritized, as was an online category for applicants with a **CGPA of 3.7 or above**. Holders of unconditional admission were warned of "longer waiting times".
  - **Aug 2024 (winter semester):** the embassy would "most likely" process only the "most deserving" applicants, meaning public scholarship holders (e.g. DAAD/HEC) and those with the best school or undergraduate results.
  - **I could not confirm whether the 3.7 CGPA category or the DAAD/scholarship priority still exists in the 2026 portal.** Unverified blogs say it does.
- **Current wait times [UNVERIFIED]:** No official figure exists. Blogs report anything from 4–8 weeks (for priority cases) to 10–12+ months (for standard cases) in Islamabad. Plan as if it could take **6–12 months**: apply in the portal **as soon as you have your admission letter**. For an October 2027 start, aim for about **Jan–Apr 2027**, which means applying to universities in late 2026 or very early 2027.

### 4d. Processing time and documents
- **Processing time after the appointment [UNVERIFIED]:** The official page gives no figure. In general, national visas can take several weeks, and longer if the Ausländerbehörde (foreigners' office in Germany) is involved.
- **Visa fee [PARTLY]:** EUR 75, payable in PKR according to the embassy leaflet. Reported (not verified) to be waived for holders of German public scholarships such as DAAD.
- **Documents [PARTLY: the portal generates the final list]:**
  - **Mandatory from the start** (official page), even if the portal marks them optional: previous degree certificates (school, bachelor's), **transcripts**, and **proof of tuition payment** if applicable. Without these the registration is halted.
  - **Typical list** (embassy leaflet and checklists):
    - national visa application form
    - biometric photo
    - passport copy
    - declarations under §54/§55 AufenthG
    - previous visas and any refusals
    - **admission letter**
    - **blocked account confirmation (EUR 11,904)** or another accepted proof of funds
    - CV
    - motivation letter
    - O/A-level (or Matric/FSc) and bachelor's certificates and transcripts
    - **IELTS/TOEFL or German certificate**
    - travel health insurance (until enrolment)
    - proof of tuition funding if fees are charged
- Sources: https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/2-study-visa-seite-1676104 ; https://digital.diplo.de/studium ; https://www.dawn.com/news/1854986 (press, not official)

## 5. Work rules and pay

- **Part-time work [VERIFIED, DAAD]:**
  - Non-EU students may work **140 full days or 280 half days per year** without approval. This was raised from 120/240 by the Skilled Immigration Act reform of March 2024.
  - **Student/academic assistant jobs (HiWi) at the university** are not counted toward this limit.
  - Working more than this needs approval from the Bundesagentur für Arbeit and the Ausländerbehörde.
  - DAAD programme pages also mention a 20 hours/week guideline during the lecture period. The legal basis is §16b(3) AufenthG; I did not check the statute text directly.
- **Minimum wage [VERIFIED, bundesregierung.de]:** **EUR 13.90/hour from 1 Jan 2026**, rising to **EUR 14.60/hour from 1 Jan 2027**.
- **Mini-job limit [VERIFIED, minijob-zentrale.de]:** **EUR 603/month in 2026** (EUR 7,236/year), rising to **EUR 633/month in 2027**. It is linked to the minimum wage (min. wage x 130 / 3). Midi-job range in 2026: EUR 603.01–2,000.
- Sources: https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/work-career/side-jobs/ ; https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/aktuelles/mindestlohn-steigt-2391010 ; https://magazin.minijob-zentrale.de/neue-verdienstgrenze-2026/
