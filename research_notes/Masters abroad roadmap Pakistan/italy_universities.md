# Italy: English-taught CS / AI / Data Science / Computer Engineering master's programmes

Researched on 2026-10-18 for a Pakistani applicant (4-year BS CS/AI, CGPA about 3.0–3.5, start September 2027).

**Key to labels**
- **[V]**: verified on an official university page during this session.
- **[V-26/27]**: verified, but it is the 2026/27 pattern. The 2027/28 call was not yet published.
- **[UNVERIFIED]**: from memory or a third-party site. Check it before relying on it.

**General Italian rules**
- Most of these rules were not re-verified this session.
- Tuition at state universities is income-based (ISEE parificato). Non-EU students usually pay between a few hundred and about €4,000 a year.
- Under national law (d.lgs. 68/2012), students who win a regional DSU scholarship (borsa di studio) are exempt from tuition. They normally pay only the regional tax and the stamp duty, about €156 + €16.
- The DSU application usually opens in July and closes in August or September of the start year, so in summer 2027. It needs a family income and assets declaration from Pakistan, legalised by the Italian embassy (or translated and legalised).
- The visa route is pre-enrolment on Universitaly, with an indicative deadline of about 30 June to mid-July. A CIMEA certificate or a Declaration of Value may be requested.
- In Lombardy, each university runs its own DSU office (no separate regional agency).
