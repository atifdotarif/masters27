# Germany Group C: "Safer" English-taught MSc options (verification notes)

Researched: 2026-10-18. Target intake: Winter Semester 2027/28.
Applicant: 4-year BS CS, FAST-NUCES, CGPA 3.41/4; about 1.5–2 years as an AI/LLM engineer; no IELTS, no GRE. Wants to use an MOI letter, with Duolingo or PTE as fallback.

Legend: **[V]** = verified on an official university page. **[DAAD]** = from the DAAD/study-guide.com listing (data supplied by the university, but second-hand). **[3P]** = third-party aggregator only. **[UNVERIFIED]** = no official confirmation found within the research budget (about 25 tool calls).

## Grade conversion (applies to every row)
Modified Bavarian formula: German grade = 1 + 3 × (Nmax − Nd) / (Nmax − Nmin).
- If uni-assist takes Nmin = 2.0 (the usual FAST minimum CGPA): 1 + 3 × 0.59 / 2 = **≈ 1.9**.
- If it takes Nmin = 1.0: **≈ 1.6**.
- Either way he clears every grade cutoff found: 2.3 (OVGU DKE, Rhine-Waal), 2.5 (OVGU DigiEng, Würzburg direct admission), and below 3.0 (Paderborn).
- uni-assist's VPD sets the official figure, so re-check once it is issued.

## Headline findings
1. **The MOI letter is officially accepted at only one programme: Würzburg AI** ("proof of English as a medium of instruction in your Bachelor's degree"). Paderborn and H-BRS explicitly reject MOI. OVGU's university-wide English policy exempts only degrees from native-English countries. Everywhere else it is unverified. **Plan on taking a test.** PTE Academic is the safer fallback: Paderborn and OVGU officially list it, while Duolingo is officially listed only at Paderborn.
2. **Hidden test requirements break the "no GRE" assumption at two schools:**
   - **Paderborn CS:** GRE (Q ≥ 157, AW ≥ 4.0) or dMat (≥ 50%) is required. The only exception is a "very good" Bachelor's grade. A ≈1.9 German grade is unlikely to count as "very good" (unverified threshold).
   - **Rhine-Waal IECS:** non-EHEA degree holders need TestAS (≥ 100) or GATE or GRE (Q 160 / V 145 / AW 4.5).
3. **OVGU (DKE and Digital Engineering) requires English at C1, not B2.** That means IELTS 7.0, TOEFL iBT 95 or PTE 76.
4. **Göttingen asks for C1 too** (IELTS 6.5 / TOEFL 95) **[DAAD]**, plus an online aptitude test and a possible video interview.
5. **Deadlines that come early:**
   - H-BRS WS 2027/28: 15 Nov 2026 – **15 Feb 2027** (via uni-assist).
   - Göttingen: **15 Apr 2027**.
   - RPTU: **30 Apr 2027**.
   - OVGU: **15 May 2027**.

## Recommended actions
- **Book PTE Academic and aim for 76+.** That covers C1 at OVGU (76) and Paderborn (75). For Duolingo, only Paderborn has confirmed acceptance (105).
- **Don't rely on the MOI letter**, except at Würzburg. Still get the letter from FAST, because it costs little and helps at Würzburg (and possibly Hildesheim and Fulda).
- **Apply for the uni-assist VPD early (Dec 2026 – Jan 2027).** It is needed for OVGU, Paderborn, H-BRS, Frankfurt UAS, Fulda, Rhine-Waal, and the Würzburg/THI VPD.
- **Calendar for WS 2027/28:**
