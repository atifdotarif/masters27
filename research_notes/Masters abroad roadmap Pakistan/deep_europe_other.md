# Europe (excluding Germany and Italy): deep dive for an English-taught CS/AI master's, 2027 start

Researched 2026-10-18. Data files:
- `masters-app/data/countries-europe.json` (20 entries)
- `masters-app/data/scholarships-europe-other.json` (16 entries)

Items marked `verified: true` were checked on an official page. Everything else comes from secondary sources or memory, so confirm it before acting.

## Top picks for this profile (≈€14–17k, MOI letter, no GRE, earn-abroad goal)
1. **Stipendium Hungaricum** (HEC quota, about 100 master's seats). It is fully funded and many host universities accept an MOI letter.
   - Deadline: expected 15 Jan 2027, on BOTH the HEC portal and the SH DreamApply portal.
   - Jobs: modest. Use it as an EU stepping stone.
2. **France public universities plus IDEX scholarships.**
   - Tuition is €3,950/yr in 2026-27 (official decree). Visa funds are €615/month.
   - The Paris-Saclay International Master's Scholarship (€10k) is verified official. Coordinators select by about 25 Mar.
   - Eiffel: schools submit by 6 Jan 2027, but internal deadlines fall in Oct–Nov 2026, so act now.
   - The 12-month APS job-search permit follows graduation. English rules vary by programme (some accept an MOI letter).
3. **Poland, Czechia and the Baltics.** Tuition is €2–6k and an MOI letter is often accepted.
   - The English IT jobs market is decent in Warsaw, Kraków and Prague.
   - CTU Open Informatics costs about CZK 132k/yr.
4. **Estonia.** It has the best English-only tech market among the cheap options.
   - TalTech costs €2.3–6k. Tartu CS costs €7,200 (about 10 waivers).
   - Usually IELTS.
5. **Austria.** Tuition is €726.72/semester (official).
   - Funds proof is €722.58/month if under 24, but €1,308.39/month at 24+ (≈€15.7k/yr). His age decides whether it fits.
   - TU Graz explicitly rejects a Pakistani English-taught degree as English proof, so IELTS is needed there. For TU Wien, email to ask.

## Long shots / over budget
- **ETH ESOP:** CHF 12k/semester + tuition. Deadline 1–30 Nov 2026 (official).
  - ETH accepts an MOI letter if the central administration confirms the whole bachelor's was taught ONLY in English, with stamp and signature.
  - Top-10% bar.
  - Tuition is now CHF 2,190/semester for foreigners.
  - EPFL: deadline 15 Dec / 31 Mar.
- **Ireland GOI-IES:** €10k + fee waiver, 60 awards. The 2026 call ran 29 Jan – 12 Mar, so expect a similar window in 2027.
  - English jobs 5/5 and a 2-year Stamp 1G.
- **UK:** UEL officially accepts an MOI letter.
  - The cheapest MSc with maintenance funds is about £25k+, which is over budget.
  - The Graduate Route drops to 18 months from Jan 2027.
- **Belgium:** KU Leuven costs about €9.5k/yr and is rising. Master Mind is open to Pakistan (general pool).
- **Netherlands:** Tuition is €18–25k and IELTS is needed. The NL Scholarship is only €5k.
- **Finland:** 100% waivers make it feasible. Funds are €800/month and there is a 2-year post-study permit. IELTS is needed. Joint application is about 7–21 Jan 2027.

## Dead ends
- **Swedish Institute SI Scholarship for Global Professionals:** Pakistan is NOT among the 34 eligible countries (verified on si.se, page fetched Oct 2026).
- **Norway:** tuition since 2023 is about €13–21k.
- **Denmark:** €10–17k.
- **Slovenia:** a small market.
