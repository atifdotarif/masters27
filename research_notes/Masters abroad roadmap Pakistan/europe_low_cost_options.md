# Low-cost and funded English-taught master's in Europe (CS/AI/Data Science) for a Pakistani student, Fall 2027 intake

Profile used: Atif Arif, BSc CS from FAST-NUCES Karachi (2022–2026), CGPA 3.41/4. Works as AI Software Engineer I at Imperium Dynamics (03/2026–now). Before that: Junior AI Engineer at SharkStack (07/2025–03/2026) and two internships. Holds Microsoft certifications AZ-104, DP-600, DP-700 and DP-800, plus GH-300. FYP was multi-modal deep learning for retinal disease (source: D:\Atif\Masters\Atif-Arif-Resume.pdf). By the time Fall 2027 applications close (around Jan–Mar 2027) he will have roughly 1.5 years of full-time AI engineering work.

Budget: under PKR 30 lakh total. **Exchange rate used: €1 = PKR 320** (OFX showed €1 = PKR 321.03 on 5 Aug 2026 and Clock7 showed PKR 317.37 on 12 Jul 2026; I found no October 2026 EUR quote. USD/PKR was 277.4/278.2 on 12 Oct 2026) — [OFX](https://www.ofx.com/en-us/exchange-rates/eur-to-pkr), [Clock7](https://www.clock7.com/currency/eur-to-pkr/), [Pak Observer](https://pakobserver.net/?p=781369). ## 1. Headline finding: the visa proof-of-funds rule decides affordability

### Takeaway
At €1 = PKR 320, the 2026–27 proof-of-funds amount for **Germany (€11,904 ≈ PKR 38.1 lakh)** and **Italy (€10,179.85/yr ≈ PKR 32.6 lakh)** is each above the whole PKR 30 lakh budget, even before tuition and flights. Self-funded routes to these two countries therefore do not fit the budget. They only work when an award letter (EMJM, DSU, university scholarship) covers all or part of the financial proof, or when the family can show more funds than the PKR 30 lakh it plans to spend. The "€6–7k for Italy" figure in the brief is out of date.

### Cited Findings
- **Italy:** For 2026–27 and 2027–28, the Type D study visa requires €10,179.85 per year, up from about €6,000 before. This excludes tuition and accommodation. — [Embassy of Italy Bucharest](https://ambbucarest.esteri.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/University-Sudy.doc); [Embassy of Italy Lusaka](https://amblusaka.esteri.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Requirements-for-Study-Visas.pdf); [TechJuice (PK)](https://www.techjuice.pk/italy-raises-student-visa-financial-requirement-to-e10179-for-2026/); [Eduvision (PK)](https://www.eduvision.edu.pk/edu_news/italy-tightens-study-visa-type-d-financial-requirements-for-international-students-news-4367)
- Italy's monthly equivalent is €848.32 per month for the whole stay. — [VFS India checklist](https://visa.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/italy/india/kolkata/english/pdf/checklist-study-short-term-new-2026.pdf)
- Italy proof of funds: cash is not accepted, and funds must be traceable. Family support up to the 4th degree of relation is accepted with documentation. A scholarship counts only with an official award letter. — [Embassy of Italy Lusaka](https://amblusaka.esteri.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Requirements-for-Study-Visas.pdf)
- **Germany:** The blocked account is €11,904 per year (€992/month), unchanged since WS 2024. A higher €1,091/month applies to Studienkolleg, language-course and Chancenkarte applicants. — [Expatrio](https://www.expatrio.com/about-germany/blocked-amount-2025); [bankdaten.de](https://www.bankdaten.de/ratgeber/sperrkonto.html). These are commercial sources; I could not retrieve the Auswärtiges Amt or German Embassy Islamabad page (the URL I tried returned 404). One Pakistani guide cites €11,208, which is the older pre-2024 figure. — [thepakistanconnect](https://thepakistanconnect.com/germany-student-visa-pakistan-passport/)

### Inferences
- In PKR at 320: Germany €11,904 = **PKR 3,809,280**; Italy €10,179.85 = **PKR 3,257,552**.
- The budget only works if (a) living costs are covered by a stipend (EMJM, DSU, DAAD) and the award letter counts toward the visa proof, or (b) the family can temporarily show more than PKR 30 lakh in traceable funds (e.g. a parent's account plus a sponsorship affidavit for Italy) while actually spending less after arrival thanks to a DSU grant or part-time work.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve an official German government page confirming the 2026/27 blocked-account amount. Check auswaertiges-amt.de or pakistan.diplo.de.
- I could not confirm whether the Islamabad Italian embassy applies €10,179.85 exactly as other missions do. Its Pakistan checklist was not retrieved.

## 2. Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters (EMJM): AI, data science, CS and cybersecurity

### Takeaway
EMJM is the only route here that clearly covers everything: tuition, travel and insurance, plus a €1,400/month stipend for 12–24 months (up to €33,600). Out-of-pocket cost is close to zero beyond the visa, documents and a cash buffer. Deadlines for Fall 2027 mostly fall in **Dec 2026–Jan 2027**, and some open as early as Oct–Nov 2026, so applications should start **now**.

### Cited Findings
- The EMJM scholarship is €1,400/month for up to 24 months. Glasgow-run EMJMs offer an option that pays an advance for travel/visa costs, after which the monthly amount is €1,275; the other option pays €1,400 with no advance. — [Univ. of Glasgow EMJM scholarships page](https://www.gla.ac.uk/postgraduate/erasmusmundus/magma/scholarships/); [Glasgow EduMAH](https://www.gla.ac.uk/postgraduate/erasmusmundus/edumah/scholarships)
- 2027 guides repeat the €1,400/month figure and say most EMJMs take applications between October 2026 and January 2027. — [Collegedunia](https://collegedunia.com/study-abroad/article/erasmus-mundus-joint-masters-2027-scholarship-guide-for-indian-students-eligibility-stipend-and-how-to-apply); [OpportunityInside](https://opportunityinside.com/scholarships/erasmus-mundus-joint-masters-eu-2027.html) (aggregators)
- The European Commission selected 37 new EMJMs for the 2027 intake. They open for student applications in autumn 2026. — [Global South Opportunities](https://www.globalsouthopportunities.com/2026/09/27/erasmus-2/); [ScholarAvenues list](https://scholaravenues.com/erasmus-mundus-programmes-2027/)
- **Specific programmes found:**
  - **EMAI – Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in Artificial Intelligence** (coordinated by UPF Barcelona). For the 2027–2029 cohort, the first application period runs **15 Nov 2026 – 5 Jun 2027 (23:59 CEST)** for worldwide self-funded candidates. A second window, 6 Jun – 6 Jul 2027, is for EU self-funded candidates only. Decisions are rolling. — [UPF EMAI official page](https://www.upf.edu/web/emai/access-admission). *The excerpt covered the self-funded windows; the EMJM scholarship-track deadline was not visible in what I retrieved and is likely earlier (around Jan 2027). Check the page.*
  - **CoDaS – Communications and Data Science.** For 2027–2029, applications are reported to open **1 Dec 2026** with a main deadline of **5 Jan 2027**. — [ScholarshipOpenings](https://scholarshipopenings.com/erasmus-mundus-codas/) (aggregator, unverified)
  - **DEAI – Data Engineering and AI.** The last window was 21 Oct 2025 – 13 Jan 2026; the 2027 dates were not yet published. — [Erasmus Catalogue](https://erasmuscatalogue.com/deai-erasmus-mundus/); [tuniversite](https://tuniversite.com/en/erasmus-mundus-joint-master-deai/)

### Inferences
- **First-year cost with an EMJM scholarship:** about €300–800 out of pocket (visa fee, document attestation/HEC, IELTS if needed, first-month buffer). That is about **PKR 1–2.5 lakh**, well within budget. The stipend covers living costs, and the scholarship includes a travel/installation and insurance contribution (standard EMJM practice, though not individually sourced here).
- Other AI/DS/cyber EMJMs worth checking in the official catalogue (names from general knowledge, not verified for 2027 in this session): EMJMD in Big Data Management & Analytics (BDMA), EIT/EMJM Cybersecurity (CYBERUS), Japan-Europe Master on Advanced Robotics, MIR (Intelligent Robotics), SE4GD (Software Engineers for Green Deal), IMLEX, Green Networking & Cloud Computing (GENIAL), Applied Data Science/ADS etc. The report writer should treat these as leads, not verified facts.
- **Chance assessment (CGPA 3.41 from FAST):** EMJM selection is very competitive. Admission itself is reachable (a FAST CS degree is well regarded), but **scholarship-holder** places are typically won by applicants with 3.6+ GPA, research output or very strong motivation. Atif's 1.5 years of production AI work (MCP, LangGraph, RAG, voice agents), four Microsoft certifications and a medical-imaging DL FYP are real differentiators for applied-AI programmes like EMAI and DEAI. Estimated chance of a scholarship from any single EMJM: **low to moderate (about 5–15%)**. Applying to 3 EMJMs (the usual maximum) gives a meaningfully better combined chance. These numbers are judgement, not sourced statistics.

### Gaps
- There is no official EU catalogue extract with 2027 deadlines for each AI/DS/cyber EMJM. The catalogue to check is https://www.eacea.ec.europa.eu/scholarships/erasmus-mundus-catalogue_en.
- I found no confirmed per-programme acceptance-rate data.

## 3. Italy: regional DSU scholarships plus English-taught CS/AI master's

### Takeaway
Italy is the best **self-applied, income-based** route. DSU grants are need-based (ISEE parificato below about €28.3k), not GPA-gated, so a 3.41 CGPA is not a barrier. The two main hurdles are (1) the new **€10,179.85 visa proof of funds** and (2) the slow document chain: Universitaly pre-enrolment, then embassy legalization of income documents, then the CAF ISEE parificato. DSU money only arrives after enrolment (the first instalment is typically around Dec–Jan), so the family must be able to show and bridge the visa funds first.

### Cited Findings
- **DSU Toscana (Pisa, Florence, Siena) 2026/27:** the bachelor's and master's window ran **20 Jul – 7 Sep 2026**, and the PhD round runs 25 Sep – 16 Nov 2026. Non-EU students use an ISEE parificato. Selection is a ranking by financial need plus merit. — [afterschoolafrica](https://www.afterschoolafrica.com/86224/university-of-pisa-dsu-scholarship/); [european-funding-guide](https://www.european-funding-guide.eu/scholarship/dsu-toscana-scholarship-borsa-di-studio-dsu-scholarship-at-university-of-pisa); [studyabroad.pk](https://www.studyabroad.pk/news-event/university-of-siena-dsu-scholarship-2026-27-in-italy-25971) (secondary sources)
- The national ISEE ceiling for 2026/27 is about €28,339.88. — [ScholarshipUnion guide](https://guides.scholarshipunion.com/italy-dsu/) (secondary)
- **DSU amounts conflict across sources:** "up to €6,806/yr", "€2,850–8,300/yr by region/category", and "~€8,250 top off-site package". The amount depends on the ISEE band and status (off-site, commuter or at home), plus a free meal or housing allowance and a tuition waiver. — [OpportunityInside](https://opportunityinside.com/scholarships/dsu-scholarship-italy-2026.html); [ScholarshipUnion](https://guides.scholarshipunion.com/italy-dsu/). I did not retrieve the official dsu.toscana.it, er-go.it, laziodisco.it or edisu.piemonte.it 2026/27 calls.
- **ISEE parificato procedure:** For 2026/27, family income from **2024** is used. Documents are uploaded **only through the university's CAF web portal**. Students from countries outside the Venice list's groups A/B/C need documents issued in the home country and **legalized by the Italian Embassy/Consulate**. Group B (Apostille countries) only need an Apostille. — [Ca' Foscari ISEE parificato 2026/27 doc list](https://www.unive.it/pag/fileadmin/user_upload/ateneo/diritto-allo-studio/tasse/DOCUMENTS_REQUIRED_FOR_ISEE_PARIFICATO_2026_2027.pdf); [Ca' Foscari FAQ 2026](https://www.unive.it/pag/fileadmin/user_upload/ateneo/diritto-allo-studio/tasse/FAQ_2026.pdf); [Univ. Perugia doc list](https://www.unipg.it/files/pagine/1996/documents-for-the-request-iseeu-parificato_en.pdf)
- Pakistan is **not** on the "particular difficulty" list (which includes Nepal and Sri Lanka) that allows documents to be obtained from foreign missions in Italy. — [Ca' Foscari 2026/27](https://www.unive.it/pag/fileadmin/user_upload/ateneo/diritto-allo-studio/tasse/DOCUMENTS_REQUIRED_FOR_ISEE_PARIFICATO_2026_2027.pdf)
- Documents typically used by Pakistani applicants (non-official source): FBR income certificates or tax returns for each earning family member, a NADRA Family Registration Certificate, a property certificate (or a "no property" certificate), and bank statements. These are translated into Italian and legalized. — [OpportunityInside](https://opportunityinside.com/blog/dsu-scholarship-italy-2026.html)
- **Universitaly / visa in Pakistan:** Students must complete Universitaly pre-enrolment, and only applications validated by the university are eligible. BLS then contacts the student for a visa appointment, allocated by university validation date. The 2026 cycle's visa submission deadline was **30 Nov 2026**. Booking goes through the official waiting-list portal (reported as "Intiana"), and the embassy warns against buying appointments from agents. — [Embassy of Italy Islamabad](https://ambislamabad.esteri.it/en/?p=3034); [ProPakistani 26 Jul 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/07/26/italy-issues-new-visa-appointment-guidelines-for-pakistanis/); [ARY News](https://arynews.tv/italy-visa-for-pakistanis-how-to-book-an-appointment-in-2026)
- The Islamabad embassy reported an "unprecedented rise in forged documents, particularly fake proof of funds" (Sept 2026), and these can lead to refusal. — [thepakistanconnect](https://thepakistanconnect.com/italy-embassy-pakistan-visa-update/)
- **University windows (2027/28):**
  - **PoliMi:** reported master's window **1 Oct – 1 Dec 2026**, separate merit-scholarship form by **15 Dec 2026 (noon CET)**. — [scholarshipsforafricans](https://scholarshipsforafricans.com/scholarships/politecnico-di-milano-merit-based-international-scholarships) (aggregator)
  - **PoliTo:** in 2026/27 the non-EU master's calls closed **13 Mar 2026 (14:00)** and **17 Apr 2026**. The first call usually opens in December and the second in March. — [PoliTo official](https://www.polito.it/en/education/applying-studying-graduating/admissions-and-enrolment/master-s-degree-programmes/applicants-with-a-non-italian-qualification)
  - **Bologna:** the 2027/28 call is expected to open in late 2026, with non-EU deadlines typically Mar–May. **Padova:** expected to open around Feb 2027. — [LinkedIn post](https://kr.linkedin.com/in/milon-mia-021129224) (unverified)
  - The Dichiarazione di Valore or equivalent from the Italian consulate takes several weeks. — [studyineurope.eu](https://www.studyineurope.eu/pt/study-in-italy/application-deadlines/)

### Inferences
- **Pakistan joined the Hague Apostille Convention (in force for Pakistan from March 2023, from my background knowledge, not verified in this session).** If the CAF or university accepts it, MOFA apostille may replace Italian-embassy legalization for ISEE documents. Confirm with the target university's CAF before starting, because the Venice list's group membership was not visible.
- **Suggested target programmes (names from general knowledge; English-taught):** PoliMi MSc Computer Science & Engineering; PoliTo MSc Computer Engineering / Data Science & Engineering; Padova MSc Computer Engineering / Data Science; Bologna MSc Artificial Intelligence; Sapienza MSc Engineering in Computer Science / Data Science; Pisa MSc Computer Science (AI curriculum) / Data Science & Business Informatics; Trento MSc Computer Science / AI Systems. Exact 2027 windows need checking on each site.
- **Estimated first-year cost, Italy with a full DSU grant (off-site student):**
  - **Fees and admin:**
    - Application fees: €0–100 (PoliMi, Padova and Bologna charge small fees, roughly €30–60 each; not sourced).
    - Document legalization/translation/HEC/IBCC: about PKR 50–80k (estimate).
    - Visa: €50 national D-visa fee (standard, not sourced here) plus BLS service charge.
    - Tuition: €0–156 regional tax, waived for DSU winners.
  - **Insurance and living:**
    - Health insurance: about €150–250/yr private policy before the SSN enrolment of about €700 (estimate; not sourced).
    - Living: €8–10k/yr in Milan/Turin/Bologna, partly offset by a DSU cash grant of about €5–7k plus a meal/housing benefit.
  - **Travel:** one-way flight KHI to Milan or Rome about PKR 1.5–2.5 lakh (estimate; not sourced).
  - **Net out-of-pocket cash outflow in year 1 if DSU is won:** about €3,000–4,500 (**PKR 10–14 lakh**). This fits the budget, **but** the visa needs €10,179.85 (PKR 32.6 lakh) shown in traceable family funds, because the DSU award is not confirmed before the visa. Many DSU rankings are published Sep–Oct, after visa submission.
- **Chance assessment (3.41 CGPA):**
  - Admission to Padova, Pisa, Trento, Sapienza or Bologna CS/AI master's: **high to moderate**.
  - PoliMi CSE: moderate. PoliMi ranks on GPA and university reputation.
  - PoliTo: moderate to high.
  - DSU grant: **high, if family ISEE parificato is below about €24–28k**, which most Pakistani middle-class families meet in EUR terms. DSU is need-ranked, and in Tuscany and Lombardy most eligible students receive it (unverified pattern).
  - PoliMi/Padova international merit scholarships: low to moderate at 3.41.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve exact 2026/27 DSU amount tables from the official ER.GO, Lazio DiSCo, EDISU Piemonte or DSU Toscana calls.
- I found no published Islamabad backlog or processing-time figure. Reports describe a validation-date-based queue, not a numeric wait.
- Whether Italian missions in Pakistan accept a DSU or university award letter toward the €10,179.85 at the time of the visa is not confirmed. The Lusaka embassy accepts award letters in general.

## 4. Germany: tuition-free English-taught programmes

### Takeaway
Germany is tuition-free at most public universities, apart from TUM's non-EU fees and Baden-Württemberg's €1,500 per semester. In practice it is **unaffordable on a PKR 30 lakh self-funded budget**, because the €11,904 blocked account (≈ PKR 38 lakh) must be deposited before the visa. Germany works only with a DAAD/EPOS or university scholarship, or a formal sponsor (Verpflichtungserklärung).

### Cited Findings
- All 16 states charge no tuition at public universities, with the key exception of Baden-Württemberg (about €1,500/semester for non-EU students). The semester contribution is about €150–350 (another source says €300–700/yr). — [gabble.ai](https://gabble.ai/blog/cost-of-masters-degree-in-germany) (aggregator)
- There are about 87 English-taught CS master's programmes, about 69% of them tuition-free for non-EU students. Example: **TU Hamburg MSc Computer Science**, English, 4 semesters, €0 tuition. — [MyGermanUniversity](https://www.mygermanuniversity.com/subjects/computer-science/master/english-programs)
- The blocked account is €11,904/yr (€992/month). — [Expatrio](https://www.expatrio.com/about-germany/blocked-amount-2025)
- **APS for Pakistan: sources conflict.** Visaflow and ApplyBoard list APS countries as India, China, Vietnam (and Mongolia) and say Pakistan is **not** included. — [Visaflow](https://www.visaflow.app/blog/do-pakistanis-need-aps-certificate-germany); [ApplyBoard](https://assist.applyboard.com/hc/en-us/articles/48637041465357). Pakistani outlets claim APS or an "Academic Evaluation Centre" certificate is mandatory. — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/perspective/germany-sees-14-rise-in-pakistani-students-over-two-years/); [thepakistanconnect](https://thepakistanconnect.com/germany-student-visa-pakistan-passport/). Either way, HEC/IBCC attestation of degrees is expected. — [thepakistanconnect](https://thepakistanconnect.com/germany-student-visa-pakistan-passport/)
- The APS fee where it applies is about €225. — [visatocampus](https://visatocampus.com/aps-certificate-germany-requirements/)

### Inferences
- First-year cost, Germany self-funded (non-BW, non-TUM):
  - Blocked account €11,904
  - Semester fees about €600/yr
  - uni-assist fee about €75 for the first application plus €30 each additional (standard uni-assist fees, not re-verified)
  - Visa €75
  - Insurance €0 up front if public insurance is paid monthly from the blocked account; about €140/month (not re-verified)
  - Flight about €550
  - **Total cash needed up front ≈ €13,200 ≈ PKR 42 lakh**, about 1.4 times the budget. The blocked account is the student's own living money, released monthly, but it must be fully funded first.
- Germany winter-semester deadlines for non-EU applicants are typically **15 Jan – 31 May 2027** (via uni-assist, e.g. TU Hamburg, RWTH, TU Berlin, Saarland). Not individually sourced here.
- **Chance assessment (3.41):** On the German grade conversion (Modified Bavarian formula), 3.41/4 ≈ German **2.0–2.3**. This is competitive for TU Hamburg, Saarland, TU Dresden, Paderborn, Bonn and Passau. RWTH and TU Berlin are moderate. Uni Stuttgart and KIT in BW are moderate but charge BW fees. TUM Informatics is low to moderate and charges non-EU tuition. **DAAD EPOS** requires 2+ years of work experience and specific development-related programmes, so it is unlikely to fit by 2027.

### Gaps
- I found no official confirmation of whether APS or an Academic Evaluation Centre is mandatory for Pakistan in 2026/27. Check pakistan.diplo.de; the page I tried returned 404.
- I did not retrieve German embassy Islamabad/Karachi student-visa waiting times.

## 5. University merit scholarships open to Pakistanis (Nordics, Benelux, Ireland, France, Baltics, others)

### Takeaway
The realistic merit options are **Finland**, where the university "Finland Scholarship" gives a 100% waiver plus €5,000 at most universities (deadline around Jan 2027), and the **Netherlands NL Scholarship** (€5,000 one-off; not enough alone). Sweden's flagship SI Global Professionals scholarship **excludes Pakistan**. Estonia (Tartu) now charges non-EU master's fees with only a few waivers. Most other merit awards (TU Delft Justus & Louise van Effen, Twente UTS, Eiffel, KU Leuven) target GPAs well above 3.41, so they are long shots.

### Cited Findings
- **Finland:**
  - The Finland Scholarship is funded by the Ministry of Education but awarded by universities. At Aalto, Tampere, Turku, Jyväskylä, UEF, Oulu, LUT, Vaasa and Lapland the flagship package is a 100% tuition waiver plus €5,000 toward first-year living costs. Helsinki gives a 50% or 100% waiver with no cash. — [OpportunityInside](https://opportunityinside.com/scholarships/finland-scholarship-2027.html); [Tutopiya](https://www.tutopiya.com/study-abroad/scholarships/finland-scholarship/) (aggregators)
  - The joint application round for 2027 is expected to close around **20 Jan 2027** on studyinfo.fi (unconfirmed). One listing gives Aalto's master's deadline as **5 Jan 2027, 15:00 EET**, with scholarships decided from the admission application. — [OpportunityInside](https://opportunityinside.com/scholarships/finland-scholarship-2027.html); [Borderless](https://borderless.so/fr/scholarships/aalto/aalto-university-excellence-scholarship)
  - Tampere University Scholarship is listed as 50% of tuition, which conflicts with the 100% figure above. — [Tutopiya Aalto page](https://www.tutopiya.com/study-abroad/scholarships/aalto-university-scholarships/)
- **Sweden:** The SI Scholarship for Global Professionals is open to citizens of 33–34 listed countries (Bangladesh, Indonesia, Egypt and others). **Pakistan is not on the list.** It requires 3,000 hours of work experience. — [Al-Fanar](https://al-fanarmedia.org/scholarships/swedish-institute-scholarships-for-global-professionals-2/); [OpportunityInside](https://opportunityinside.com/scholarships/si-scholarship-global-professionals-sweden-2027.html)
- **Netherlands:** The Holland Scholarship was renamed the **NL Scholarship** in June 2023. It is €5,000 for the first year only, with deadlines set by each institution (many around **1 Feb 2027**; some later, up to 1 Apr 2027). Participating institutions in 2026–27 included TU/e, Maastricht, Groningen, Twente, Utrecht, VU and Wageningen. — [indiascholarships.in](https://www.indiascholarships.in/scholarships/holland-scholarship); [opportunitiesforafricans](https://www.opportunitiesforafricans.com/holland-nl-scholarships-2026-2027/); [opportunityportal (Maastricht 2027)](https://opportunityportal.info/maastricht-university-holland-scholarship-2027/)
- **Estonia (Tartu):** MSc Computer Science tuition is **€7,200/yr for non-EU students**, with a **15 Mar** deadline and 63 places. The university says international master's study is now fee-based for non-EU starters, with fee reductions in some curricula. One listing mentions 10 waivers open to all citizenships. — [Univ. of Tartu CS](https://ut.ee/compsci); [UT news](https://ut.ee/en/news/applications-english-taught-masters-programmes-are-closing-soon-tuition-waivers-available); [Mastersportal](https://www.mastersportal.com/studies/65568/computer-science.html)
- **TalTech:** older (2023) material says tuition waivers go to the best candidates in certain programmes, with a 15 Mar deadline for non-EU applicants. — [PoliTo-hosted TalTech presentation](https://www.polito.it/sites/default/files/2023-10/OUT_erasmusdays2023_eetallinn02.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Finland with a 100% waiver plus €5k:**
  - Year-1 costs:
    - Application €0–100
    - Residence permit about €350 (online; not sourced)
    - Insurance about €400 (not sourced)
    - Flight about €550
    - Living about €9–10k in Helsinki, less in Tampere or Oulu
  - The €5k grant offsets this, so net out-of-pocket is about €5–6k ≈ **PKR 16–19 lakh**, which fits the budget.
  - The residence permit requires proof of funds (commonly cited as €800/month ≈ €9,600/yr; **not verified this session**). This is the same proof-of-funds problem as Italy and Germany.
  - Chance for a 100% waiver at 3.41: **low to moderate** at Aalto, moderate at Tampere, Oulu, LUT, Turku or UEF. Strong industry AI experience helps in the motivation letter.
- **Netherlands:** non-EU tuition is roughly €18–25k (not sourced), so NL €5k does not make it affordable. **TU Delft Justus & Louise van Effen** (full tuition plus living) is excellence-level, typically top ~5% of class or GPA 8/10+, so chances at 3.41 are **very low**. Twente UTS and TU/e Talent: low. **Not a budget route.**
