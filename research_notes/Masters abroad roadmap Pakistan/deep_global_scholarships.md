# Global, foundation and Pakistani scholarships and loans (researched 2026-10-18)

Data file: `masters-app/data/scholarships-global.json` (25 entries, valid JSON).
Applicant: Karachi (Sindh domicile), BS CS FAST 3.41, about 2 years in AI engineering, Fall 2027, Germany budget €14-17k, no IELTS/GRE, wants to earn abroad first.

## Worth pursuing (ranked)
1. **Stipendium Hungaricum via HEC**: fully funded EU master's in CS/AI, often with an MOI letter, deadline ~15 Jan 2027 (dual portal: HEC + SH). The 2026-27 deadline was 15 Jan 2026 (HEC page, verified). No Hungarian bond; check whether HEC adds an undertaking.
2. **Aga Khan Foundation ISP**: Pakistan is listed. 50% grant / 50% loan, master's priority, under-30 preference. The 2026-27 cycle is closed; the "next cycle opens early 2027" (AKDN page, verified). The international deadline is usually ~31 March, and the Karachi AKF/AKEB office may set an earlier one. The best Germany top-up, because it can cover living costs and the blocked account.
3. **Rotary Global Grant**: at least US$30k. Needs a Karachi club (District 3271) and a host club in Germany. Frame the degree under "disease prevention and treatment" using the retinal-imaging FYP. Approach clubs NOW (Oct-Dec 2026); the district's internal deadlines come months before the Foundation's 3-months-before-departure rule.
4. **HEC Overseas Phase-III**: CS/AI fits under "Digital Transformation & Emerging Technologies". Top-200 QS/THE, so TUM, KIT, RWTH and others qualify. CGPA >= 3.0, age <= 30, HAT. Batch-IV closed 28 Aug 2026; Batch-V is not announced. **Bond:** Deed of Agreement & Surety Bond + service undertaking. HEC practice is to return and serve (typically 5 years) or repay; confirm in the template. Conflicts with the earn-abroad plan.
5. **Bank Alfalah study-abroad loan**: the only Pakistani bank product found for study abroad (source: 2020 ProPakistani guide, unverified). Expensive (KIBOR-based).

## Sindh-specific
- **Bhutto STEM Scholarships at Oxford** (Sindh College Education Dept + Oxford Pakistan Programme + Lady Margaret Hall): 6 awards in 2026-27 (3 for women, 3 open merit), master's/MPhil/DPhil with STEM preferred. Needs a Sindh CNIC, domicile, PRC (Form-D), an unconditional Oxford offer and Oxford's English requirement. Announced Dec 2025; the closing date was not found. This is the only Sindh overseas award found. It needs an Oxford admit plus IELTS, which is hard with a 3.41.
- **SEEF (Sindh Educational Endowment Fund)**: domestic only (partner institutions in Sindh/Pakistan); the last confirmed deadline was 30 Sep 2024. No overseas component found.
- No "Sindh Endowment Fund for higher studies abroad" was found as a live scheme.

## Not eligible or not useful (fit low)
- JJ/WBGSP: needs 3+ years of *development* work, and CS/AI programmes are not participating programmes.
- IsDB: the M.Sc. track is only for LDMCs, which do not include Pakistan. The Merit track is PhD.
- OPEC Fund scholarship: restructuring, not accepting applications.
- Gates Cambridge / Rhodes Pakistan / Knight-Hennessy / Schwarzman / Yenching: elite, need tests, and the 2027 deadlines are mostly passed. Rhodes 2027 closed 5 Aug 2026. The Rhodes age rule is 18-23, or <27 with the first degree finished on/after 1 Oct 2025 (verified on the Rhodes House page).
- Chevening: the 2027/28 window was Aug-6 Oct 2026. 2028/29 is expected Aug-Oct 2027. **2-year return-home rule.**
- Fulbright (USEFP): the 2027 program deadline was ~1 Apr 2026, so the 2028 program is due ~Apr 2027. GRE required (~145/section), TOEFL for shortlisted. **J-1 2-year home residency.**
- Commonwealth Shared: Pakistan IS eligible (verified). The 2026 round closed 9 Dec 2025; the 2027 round is likely ~Dec 2026. Return expected, development-linked courses. Medium fit.
- Google/Microsoft/Meta/NVIDIA fellowships: PhD-only. Women Techmakers: women only and largely discontinued.
- AAUW (women), Mastercard Foundation (Africa), Zakat Foundation of America (US students), Erasmus+ ICM (current students of partner institutions only, so not after graduating). Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters IS open to Pakistanis; check it separately.
- PEEF: Punjab domicile only, domestic. HEC-USAID MNBSP: domestic, female-only recently. Ihsan Trust / Akhuwat: domestic partner-university loans only (beware of fake Akhuwat .it.com sites). Karandaaz: no student loan product. PM overseas scheme: last round 2022-23, closed.
- CSC China via HEC: deadline ~early Jan 2027, fully funded, no IELTS, but weak for the earn-abroad goal.

## Germany top-up strategy
- AKF ISP (apply ~Jan-Mar 2027) + Rotary Global Grant (start now) are the only realistic external top-ups for a German master's without a bond.
- HEC OSS would fully fund TUM/KIT/RWTH but carries a service bond.
- Germany-native top-ups (Deutschlandstipendium €300/month, DAAD EPOS needs 2 years of work in a development-linked field) are outside this file; see the Germany notes.
