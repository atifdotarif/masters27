## Q2. Eligibility for each scheme (nationality, CGPA, work experience, age, English test)

### Takeaway
Atif's 3.41 CGPA clears every academic bar found: first division for HEC/CSC, 3.0 for MIS, and 2nd-class-upper equivalents.

His binding constraints are work experience and tests:
- **Work experience:** Chevening requires 2,800 hours gained after the degree. Australia Awards and EPOS typically expect about 2 years.
- **Tests:** HAT is required for HEC-nominated schemes. GRE is required for Fulbright. IELTS or TOEFL is needed nearly everywhere.

### Cited Findings
**Chevening**
- Requires at least 2,800 hours of work experience, about 2 years full-time. It must span at least 2 years and be gained **after** the undergraduate degree. Internships, part-time and voluntary work count. — [Chevening work experience](https://www.chevening.org/scholarships/who-can-apply/work-experience/)
- No upper age limit. Funds a one-year UK master's. — [Chevening who can apply](https://chevening.org/scholarships/who-can-apply/)

**Commonwealth Master's (via HEC)**
- Pakistani or AJK national and permanent resident; dual nationals are excluded. No upper age limit.
- Requires a 16-year bachelor's with first division in a relevant field. Result-awaiting candidates are allowed.
- HAT minimum 60/100; only HAT-I to IV are accepted.
- HEC nominates 26 master's candidates (and 30 PhD), with at least 45% female.
- Source for all of the above: [HEC CW page](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/HECAnnouncements/Pages/CW-Scholarship.aspx)
- CSC English minimums: IELTS 6.5, TOEFL iBT 92, PTE 62, Duolingo 115. Only taught full-time master's are funded; MBA and research master's are not. — [ProPakistani 21 Sep 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/09/21/pakistanis-can-study-masters-and-phd-in-uk-with-2000-monthly-support-under-commonwealth-scholarships/amp/)

**Fulbright**
- Must be a Pakistani citizen residing in Pakistan; dual US/Pakistani nationals are ineligible. Requires 16 years of education.
- GRE is required, with at least 145 on each section. GRE fees are reimbursed for scores of 300+ (science) at HEC-approved public universities.
- 2 years of work experience is required only for MBA and Public Policy master's.
- Source: [USEFP](https://www.usefp.org/scholarships/fulbright-degree.cfm)

**DAAD EPOS**
- Generally expects at least 2 years of professional experience after the bachelor's; requirements vary by course. — [studyabroad.pk](https://www.studyabroad.pk/scholarships/daad-epos-scholarship-2027-in-germany-for-pakistani-students); [SpeakShark](https://speakshark.com/blog/daad-epos-2027-2028-deadlines-english-requirements)

## Q4. Post-award obligations (return-home rules), given Atif wants to work abroad after the degree

### Takeaway
Return-home rules are the deciding filter for Atif:
- **Fulbright** brings a J-1 two-year home-residency requirement plus a USEFP contract. This effectively blocks H-1B or a green card for 2 years.
- **Australia Awards** requires a 2-year return to Pakistan.
- **Chevening** is known to require 2 years at home, but this was not re-verified this cycle.

The more work-abroad-friendly schemes are the continental ones (DAAD, Türkiye, GKS, MEXT, Hungary, SI), which have no formal return bond in the sources found.

### Cited Findings
**Fulbright**
- Successful candidates sign a USEFP contract. PhD candidates also sign an HEC bond to return and serve for the length of the scholarship, redeemable year-for-year with a minimum of 2 years. — [USEFP](https://www.usefp.org/scholarships/fulbright-degree.cfm)
- J-1 holders funded wholly or partly by the US government are subject to INA 212(e). They must spend 2 years physically in their home country before they can get H, L or permanent residence. Waivers exist. — [Stony Brook 212(e)](https://www.stonybrook.edu/visa/j-1-students/other-topics/home-residency-requirement-212-e.html); [WashU 212(e)](https://oiss.washu.edu/two-year-home-residence-requirement-212e/)

**Australia Awards**
- Requires a commitment to return to Pakistan for at least 2 years after the award. — [DFAT intake info](https://www.dfat.gov.au/sites/default/files/australia-awards-pakistan-information-for-intake.pdf) (as summarised in search results)

**Commonwealth Master's**
- The HEC page does not state a return bond. — [HEC CW page](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/HECAnnouncements/Pages/CW-Scholarship.aspx)
- The CSC frames awards around development impact at home. — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/09/21/pakistanis-can-study-masters-and-phd-in-uk-with-2000-monthly-support-under-commonwealth-scholarships/amp/)

**Chevening**
- The pages fetched did not state the 2-year return rule. — [Chevening who can apply](https://chevening.org/scholarships/who-can-apply/)

**HEC Overseas**
- Scholarships usually carry a service bond in Pakistan, but this was not verified for Phase-III Batch-IV.

### Inferences
- **Chevening:** it is widely documented that scholars must return home for at least 2 years, which blocks the UK Graduate Route. This is not re-sourced here; verify in the Chevening FAQ.
- **Commonwealth:** CSC master's scholars have historically been required to return home, and sponsor consent issues may block the UK Graduate Route. This needs verification.
- **HEC-funded schemes** (Overseas, and possibly HEC-nominated CSC or SH) commonly include a bond or return service. Atif should read each agreement.
- **Best fit with his goal to work abroad:** DAAD (Germany allows an 18-month post-study job-seeker permit), Stipendium Hungaricum, Türkiye Bursları, GKS, MEXT and SI. No return bond was found for these in this research. However, their post-study work routes were not researched here.

### Gaps
- Official 2027/28 wording on return obligations for Chevening, Commonwealth, Türkiye Bursları, GKS, MEXT, SH, CSC and SISGP was not fetched.
- Whether HEC-nominated SH or CSC awards carry an HEC bond was not found.
- The Australia Awards PDF timed out on a direct fetch. The 2-year rule is taken from the search summary of that official document.
