# Italy: admin, DSU scholarships, visa, work rules (Pakistani applicant, intake Sept 2027)

Research date: 2026-10-18. All figures are for **a.a. 2026/27**, the latest published cycle. The 2027/28 calls come out around June–July 2027 and usually rise about 1–2% with ISTAT.
Legend: **[V]** verified from an official source this session. **[S]** from a secondary or non-official source only. **[U]** unverified: general knowledge, check it.

---

## 0. Key takeaways

- **Pakistan is an Apostille country.** The Italian Embassy Islamabad has accepted **only MOFA-apostilled documents since 18 Nov 2024**, so no embassy legalization is needed. You still need an Italian translation **[V]**. Watch out: some DSU bandi (DiSCo Lazio is one) still ask for income documents "issued by the Italian diplomatic authorities in the country of residence". Read each bando's foreign-documents clause **[V for Lazio wording]**.
- **National 2026/27 caps (MUR DD 176/2026):** ISEE €28,339.88 and ISPE €61,608.48. Regions may set lower caps **[S, consistent across sources; DiSCo bando confirms the same figures]**.
- **Base national fuori-sede scholarship: €7,171.11.** It rises by +15% for low ISEE in most regions and by +20% for female STEM students **[V via ER.GO, ESU Padova]**.
- **Proof of funds for the 2026/27 visa: €10,179.85/year** (€848.32/month × 12, or €783.06 × 13) **[V, Italian embassy vademecums in Podgorica and Brazzaville; the Islamabad page does not restate the figure]**.
- **2026/27 visa deadline in Islamabad: 30 Nov 2026** **[V]**. **For 2027/28, Helsinki and WAItaly quote 31 Oct 2027** **[S/V-other-embassy]**. Plan on **31 Oct 2027 or earlier** and confirm with the Islamabad notice (expected around May 2027).
- **Most DSU deadlines fall in July–September 2026,** before the student arrives. For a Sept 2027 start, the window is roughly **June–Sept 2027**, which means the ISEE parificato paperwork must be ready from Pakistan by about June 2027.

## 1. DSU regional scholarships 2026/27

General national rules, applying everywhere **[V via the bandi read]**:
- **First-year students** are ranked on economic criteria only.
- **Merit for the 2nd payment:** 20 CFU by 10 Aug of year 1. If you miss it, the scholarship is cut or revoked, and you may have to repay it.
- **Merit to win the scholarship in year 2 of a Laurea Magistrale:** **30 CFU by 10 Aug** of year 1. A bonus of credits can be used once.
- **Fuori sede status** requires a registered paid lease, or a DSU residence place, of **≥10 months** near the campus. Without one you get the lower **pendolare** amount.
- **Winners get:** full exemption from university fees and the regional tax refunded or exempted.

## 2. ISEE parificato (ISEEUP) for a Pakistani family

**Documents** (DiSCo bando wording; other regions are similar) **[V]**, all **translated into Italian**:
1. **Family composition certificate** (NADRA FRC) giving names and dates of birth of every member.
2. **Gross income of each family member for the reference year.** The 2026/27 calls use **2024 income**, so the 2027/28 calls will use **2025 income**. Write "no income" explicitly where it applies. **Total family income cannot be zero.**
3. **Real estate owned by each member at 31 Dec of the reference year, with surface area in m²** (property valuation or ownership certificate).
4. **Movable assets** (bank balances, investments) at 31 Dec of the reference year. If there are none, say so explicitly.
5. Rent paid for the family home, if any.
- No affidavits or self-declarations are accepted (Lazio).
- The family income must be **consistent with the funds declared for the visa / permesso**.

**Apostille vs legalization:**
- Pakistan acceded to the Hague Apostille Convention. The **Italian Embassy Islamabad has accepted only MOFA-apostilled documents since 18 Nov 2024** (via apostille.mofa.gov.pk) **[V]**.
- **Translation:** Islamabad offers a "per traduzione conforme" translation service (with the visa application or by email appointment). **Karachi does not translate.** Its route is local notary, then sworn translator, then MOFA apostille **[V/S]**. A sworn translation in Italy also works.
- **Caveat:**
  - Some bandi still literally require the documents to be "issued or validated by the Italian representation". DiSCo has this wording **[V]**.
  - Pakistan is **not** on the MUR "particularly poor countries" list. That list allows a simpler embassy-certificate route, so a full ISEEUP is required **[U, check the DM 176/2026 list]**.
  - **Confirm with each DSU that an apostille satisfies the clause.**

**CAF timing:**
- The ISEEUP is signed **only at a CAF partnered with the DSU or university**, usually free of charge.
- You **can usually do it after arrival.** Lazio's deadline is 10 Dec 2026 **[V]**. Padova links the ISEE to its 30 Sep deadline **[V]**.
- Many regions require the CAF appointment, or the DSU, **before the application deadline**. Booking slots fill fast in Sept–Nov **[U]**.
- **Get all Pakistani documents apostilled and translated before leaving Pakistan.**

## 3. Student visa process (Pakistan)

- **Universitaly pre-enrolment** is mandatory. The university must **validate** it before you can apply for a visa **[V]**.
  - The 2026/27 Universitaly window opened around April–May 2026.
  - For 2027/28, expect a spring 2027 opening **[U]**. Pre-enrol as soon as you have the admission letter.
- **Jurisdiction:** Islamabad accepts only applicants **permanently resident in its consular district** **[V]**. Usual split: **Karachi = Sindh and Balochistan; Islamabad = Punjab, KP, ICT, GB, AJK** **[U, confirm on conskarachi.esteri.it]**.
- **Provider and appointments (Islamabad):**
  - **BLS-Intiana** (intianaitalyvisa.com, +92 51 8490870) **[V]**.
  - For 2026/27 the embassy **allocated appointments directly**, in chronological order of the university's validation date, by email to the Universitaly address. This works like an automatic queue **[V]**.
  - No official WhatsApp, and no fees charged for booking. Report fraud to urp.islamabad@esteri.it **[V]**.
  - **Current wait times: not published [U].** Forum reports suggest several weeks to months between validation and appointment in peak season, so get validated early.
  - Karachi's provider and system **[U]**.
- **Deadline:**
  - 2026/27: **30 Nov 2026** **[V]**.
  - **2027/28: 31 Oct 2027** per the Helsinki embassy and MUR circular reporting **[S]**. Not yet announced by Islamabad.
- **Proof of funds (2026/27):**
  - **€10,179.85/year** (€848.32/month) **[V, other Italian embassies citing the MUR/MAECI Procedures]**.
  - The money can belong to the student, parents, or relatives up to the 4th degree, or come from Italian institutions with reliable guarantees. A **university or DSU grant can count** **[S, Procedures wording]**.
  - Embassies also check **where the money came from and the account history** **[S]**.
  - A **DSU award letter usually arrives after the visa interview**, since rankings come out Oct–Nov. In practice, show bank funds **[U]**.
- **Accommodation:** proof of housing (lease, residence assignment or hostel booking) is required under the Procedures **[U, standard requirement]**.
- **Insurance:** health insurance covering Italy for the visa, then SSN enrolment on arrival (≈ €700/yr) **[U]**. DiSCo co-funds SSN enrolment for later-year students (€600 DiSCo + €100 student) **[V]**.
- **Visa fee:** €50 **[S]**.
- **Refusal issues the embassy flags:**
  - Only validated pre-enrolments are accepted. Incomplete files are the applicant's responsibility. Fraudulent intermediaries are a risk **[V]**.
  - Common refusal reasons: funds inconsistent with family income, sudden large deposits, fake documents, weak academic coherence or study intent **[U, general reports]**.

## 4. Degree recognition

- **CIMEA Statement of Comparability** **[V/S, cimea-diplome.it]**:
  - **€150**, with a **60 working days** standard turnaround.
  - **Urgent: +€100 for 30 working days.**
  - A combined verification service costs €300 (ordinary track only).
  - No new requests accepted 19–26 Jul and 23–30 Aug 2026.
  - Its opinion is non-binding.
  - Most universities accept CIMEA **instead of a Dichiarazione di Valore (DoV)**.
- **Dichiarazione di Valore** **[U]**:
  - Issued by the Italian Embassy/Consulate. Requires the degree to be apostilled (MOFA, after HEC attestation) and translated.
  - Cost: consular fee plus translation.
  - Time: often weeks to months.
  - Check whether the chosen university asks for a DoV or accepts CIMEA. **Most now accept CIMEA**, and some also accept a Diploma Supplement.
- **Recommended:** HEC attestation, then MOFA apostille, then the CIMEA statement (urgent if short on time) **[U on the HEC step]**.

## 5. Working rules

- **During studies:** **20 hrs/week, up to 1,040 hrs/year** on a study permit (DPR 394/1999 art. 14) **[U, long-standing rule, not re-checked this session]**.
- **Post-study job-search permit ("permesso per attesa occupazione"):**
  - Available to holders of an Italian bachelor's, master's, PhD or 1st/2nd-level master.
  - Lasts **9–12 months**, usually issued for 12, and is **non-renewable** **[S]**.
  - Apply **before the study permit expires** (from about 2 months before). Register with the Centro per l'Impiego, then apply through the post-office kit **[S]**.
- **Conversion to a work permit:**
  - Students can convert to a work permit (subordinato or autonomo) **outside the annual quota (fuori quota)** after earning the degree in Italy **[S]**.
  - **EU Blue Card:** salary threshold of about **€36,300 gross/yr** (recent ISTAT update) **[S, not confirmed on an official Italian portal]**. The contract must last ≥6 months and meet the collective-agreement (CCNL) minimum **[S]**.

| Pisa | €350–500 | €750–1,000 |
| Trento | €400–550 | €800–1,050 |

- **The visa minimum (€848/month) is below the real cost in Milan, Rome and Bologna.**
- **Job market for English-only speakers:**
  - Milan has most of Italy's English-friendly tech roles: multinationals, fintech, consulting, some startups. Elsewhere there is very little.
  - Italian tech salaries are low by EU standards: about **€28–38k gross for juniors in Milan**, so often below the Blue Card threshold.
  - Most SMEs and public-sector roles require **Italian (B1–B2+)**.
  - Realistic plan: learn Italian to B1–B2 during the master's, use internships (tirocini) and university career fairs (PoliMi Career Service), and target international firms.
  - English-only is feasible but narrow. Expect Germany, the Netherlands or Ireland to be easier markets for English-only tech jobs.
