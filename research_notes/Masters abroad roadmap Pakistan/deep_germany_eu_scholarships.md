# Germany + EU-wide scholarships: deep dive (researched 2026-10-18)

Applicant: Pakistani, BS CS FAST-NUCES (3.41), ~1.5–2 years in AI engineering. Wants an Oct 2027 start, English only, prefers MOI over IELTS, no GRE.
Data file: `masters-app/data/scholarships-germany-eu.json` (22 entries).

## Key conclusions
1. **The DAAD "Study Scholarships – Master Studies for All Academic Disciplines" is NOT offered for Pakistan.** It is not on the DAAD Pakistan programme list. The official entry confirms €992/month, that funding must begin in 2027, and that the degree must be no more than 6 years old. Its English proof list covers IELTS, TOEFL, PTE and Duolingo, with no MOI.
2. **The DAAD routes for Pakistan are EPOS and Helmut-Schmidt, and neither fits CS/AI.** The official EPOS 2027/28 list (status 06/2026) has no CS/AI/data course; the closest is HfT Stuttgart Photogrammetry & Geoinformatics. Deadlines are per course. Helmut-Schmidt (public policy) closes at the end of July and has already closed for 2027.
3. **The best AI-specific funding comes from the Konrad Zuse Schools of AI (DAAD-funded).**
   - **relAI (TUM/LMU):** an MSc scholarship whose official page accepts "proof that University degree was taught mainly in English" as an alternative to C1, so an MOI works. The 2026 call is closed and the next call date is not yet published (a third party says 22 Nov 2026, unverified). Students must also gain admission to a TUM/LMU MSc, where deadlines are usually end of May.
   - **ELIZA (TU Darmstadt, Freiburg, Saarland and others):** master's scholarships at DAAD rates, with reported cut-offs of 25 April and 25 October. Unverified, because the official site is JS-only.
   - Also check SECAI (TU Dresden / Leipzig), which has a similar master's track (not researched).
4. **Deutschlandstipendium (€300/month)** is open to all nationalities. The key issue is whether the window falls before or after enrolment:
   - After arrival or open to incoming students (good): TU Darmstadt (21 Sep–18 Oct 2026; master's students must be in the 1st or 2nd semester), TU Dortmund (15 Aug–15 Sep 2026; first-years eligible), Göttingen (1–30 Sep 2026; apply after enrolling), Potsdam/HPI (from 1 Jul for 3 weeks; prospective students explicitly allowed), Würzburg (deadline 16 Jul 2026; first-years eligible).
   - Before arrival, with eligibility uncertain: Passau (4 May–15 Jun 2026; reopens May 2027) and Hildesheim (June; ask stipend@uni-hildesheim.de).
   - Enrolled students only: Frankfurt UAS (1–20 May 2026; next window announced Mar/Apr 2027), so in practice only from year 2.
5. **HPI international M.Sc. CS scholarship:** €1,100/month for 4 semesters for graduates whose first degree is from outside Germany. The last confirmed calls were in 2024 (deadlines 12 May 2024 and 8 Nov 2024). The current HPI scholarship page lists only bachelor's funds, so email master-info@hpi.de.
6. **28DIGITAL (ex-EIT Digital) Master School:** the 2027/28 portal opens on 30 Oct 2026, and Period 1 for non-EU students closes on **31 Jan 2027**. The application fee is €75.
   - Non-EU students get only full or half tuition waivers. The Scholarships of Excellence with a living allowance are EU/EEA-only.
   - Some partner universities fund non-EU students: ELTE up to €10k/yr plus €1k travel, and BME up to €3.5k/semester.
   - English proof is set by each university. Aalto requires TOEFL 92 or IELTS 6.5 and says a university letter is not sufficient. Pakistan is not on the list of exempt countries.
7. **Political and religious foundations:** these are mostly impractical because they need German and/or prior enrolment.
   - **Böll:** open to applicants abroad before the master's, with priority for DAC countries. It needs German B2/DSH. Deadlines are 1 Mar 2027 (window 15 Jan–1 Mar) and 1 Sep 2027. The official page warns that from 1 Mar 2027 it will "only accept applications for first-cycle programs", so confirm this.
   - **KAS Ausländerförderung:** for non-EU graduates doing a master's. One deadline per year (15 July). German is required.
   - **FES, RLS, FNF:** have non-EU programmes, but need enrolment in Germany and German.
   - **Hans Böckler, Hanns Seidel, Avicenna:** limited to EU citizens and §8 BAföG residents, so he is ineligible.
   - **KAAD:** works by nomination via a Catholic parish or partner committee and is return-oriented.
8. **Companies and research institutes:** Bayer, Siemens, Max Planck and Helmholtz have no verified master's funding for this profile. Use HiWi or working-student jobs instead.
9. **EIT Health, EIT Manufacturing and similar:** none was researched in depth. Their master's labels are mostly EU-funded and their scholarships are often EU-only. They are low priority compared with 28DIGITAL.

## Action calendar
- **Nov 2026:** watch for the relAI MSc call. Apply to 28DIGITAL (by 31 Jan 2027, booking IELTS/TOEFL if going that route).
- **Jan–Mar 2027:** Böll (if learning German). Apply for TUM/LMU/TU Darmstadt/HPI admissions.
- **Apr 2027:** ELIZA cut-off on 25 Apr.
- **May–Jul 2027:** Deutschlandstipendium windows at Passau (May–Jun), Hildesheim (Jun), Potsdam/HPI (1–~22 Jul) and Würzburg (mid-Jul). KAS on 15 Jul.
- **Aug–Oct 2027:** Deutschlandstipendium at Dortmund (15 Aug–15 Sep), Göttingen (Sep) and TU Darmstadt (late Sep–mid Oct).
