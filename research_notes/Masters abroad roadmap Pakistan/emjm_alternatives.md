# EMJM alternatives (excluding EMAI, CoDaS, DEAI): Sept 2027 intake

Researched 2026-10-18. Applicant: BS CS FAST-NUCES (3.41/4), 1.5–2 yrs AI/LLM engineer, no IELTS, no GRE.
Legend: **[V]** = verified on the official programme page this session; **[U]** = unverified (no official confirmation found / page silent / site down).

## Key takeaways

1. **MOI letter accepted (Pakistani degree OK):** only **MAIA** [V] and **IFRoS** [V] (both run by the Univ. of Girona office). Both require an official university letter stating English was the medium of instruction.
2. **EDISS and CYBERSURE accept an English-taught degree only from a fixed list of countries** (AU, CA, NZ, UK, US, EU/EEA, CH). A Pakistani MOI letter does **not** qualify. Both say only the listed tests (IELTS/TOEFL/Cambridge) are accepted, which rules out PTE and Duolingo.
3. **PTE/Duolingo secondary list:** none of the programmes checked accepts Duolingo. IFRoS accepts **PTE General Level 3** but not PTE Academic. MAIA's test table is an image I could not read, so its PTE status is [U]. Practically, **an IELTS Academic 6.5 (writing ≥6) unlocks EDISS + CYBERSURE** and is worth booking before December 2026.
4. **GRE:** not required by any programme checked. CYBERSURE lists GRE/GMAT as optional.
5. **Scholarship amount:** the standard EMJM scholarship covers tuition, travel, and insurance, plus a living allowance of **€1,400/month** for the full programme. This is the EACEA standard rate, but **none of the programme pages fetched states the amount [U per programme]**.
6. **The EACEA catalogue cannot yet be filtered for 2027-start programmes** (its year filter stops at 2025). The 37 new EMJMs announced on 16 July 2026 for a Sept 2027 start include **MAIA** (re-funded), **MICROSCOM** (Advanced Microscopy with AI, Spain-coordinated, no website yet), **EMILDAI** (Law, Data & AI, Ireland), and **NavigAIte EU** (AI business transformation, Finland). None of them is a pure CS, SE, robotics or cybersecurity programme (source: erasmuscatalogue.com, a secondary source).

- Email IFRoS, JEMARO and CYBERUS to confirm whether 2027 EM scholarships exist.
