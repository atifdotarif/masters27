## Key verified facts (official pages)
- **KAUST**: every admitted student gets the Fellowship (tuition, stipend, housing, health insurance, relocation), with no separate application and no fee. GRE not required. Minimum GPA 3.0, but 90% of admits are above 3.3 and 75% above 3.5. **MOI certificates and Duolingo are explicitly not accepted, and there are no waivers.** IELTS 7.0 (6.5 in every band; IELTS Online accepted) or TOEFL 5/6 on the new scale. Fall 2027 window 28 Sep 2026 to 3 Jan 2027; decisions 25 Feb; start 29 Aug 2027. The stipend figure is not published (reported at about USD 20k/yr).
- **MBZUAI**: the site blocks automated access (Cloudflare), so these facts come from official-site search snippets plus ProPakistani (Sep 2026). Portal opened 1 Sep 2026, final deadline 15 Dec 2026, decisions by 31 Mar 2027. **Big change for Fall 2027: MSc admission does not include funding.** You need a merit award, a faculty RA/TA or a government scholarship. Funded students get tuition, a stipend (an older page said AED 8,000/month), housing, insurance and airfare. English: IELTS 6.5 / TOEFL 90 / DET 120. The waiver needs a degree from a country where English is an official AND the dominant language of higher education, so ask whether Pakistan counts. Acceptance rate: not found.
- **HBKU**: international deadline 1 Feb 2027 (opened 1 Oct 2026). STEM master's in CSE: 75% tuition waiver + QAR 7,000/month for up to 21 months + airfare. Competitive, and you must reapply each year.
- **Khalifa University MRTS**: full tuition + stipend + flights + medical, but **no housing**. IELTS 6.5 / TOEFL 91. English-medium graduates still need IELTS 6.0. Interview. Fall 2027 dates not yet posted.
- **AUS**: assistantship gives a tuition waiver of up to 9 credit hours per semester and/or up to AED 20,000 per semester, for 4 semesters. Does not cover housing or insurance.
- **Qatar University**: the assistantship pays only an hourly rate; the student pays tuition and housing. Weak.
- **HKUST PGS**: HK$19,135/month (2025/26); HK$229,620/yr (2026/27); up to 2 years for MPhil.
- **Schwarzman**: **MOI accepted** (2 or more years in English at your undergraduate institution), age under 29. But the next intake is Aug 2028 (applications Apr–Sep 2027) and it is not CS.
- **AIT**: ADB-JSP and RTG deadlines for the Aug 2027 intake are both 31 Mar 2027. Pakistan is an ADB borrowing member, so it is eligible for ADB-JSP.
- **JDS Pakistan**: 16 seats, government officers only, closes 27 Nov 2026. Not applicable.
- **NTU Taiwan**: 2027 September entry first round opened 29 Sep 2026.
- **MOE Taiwan**: the regional TECO's 2025 call covered only Middle East and North African countries. No Pakistan route found.

## Recommendation
1. **Apply to KAUST (by 3 Jan) and MBZUAI (by 15 Dec)**, and book one IELTS sitting now (target 7.0, for KAUST). One test unlocks both, plus HBKU and Khalifa. These offer the best earn-then-return value: tax-free Gulf salaries 2–3 hours from Karachi, and no bond.
2. **HBKU (by 1 Feb)** is the Gulf backup.
3. **GKS University Track (Feb–Mar 2027)** and **HKUST MPhil** are the strongest no-IELTS-maybe routes.
4. **ADB-JSP at AIT (31 Mar)** works only if you have 24 or more months of full-time work. It expects you to return home, which is acceptable given the long-term plan.
5. **CSC Type B** is a safety net. It is easy to win but weak for earning afterwards.
