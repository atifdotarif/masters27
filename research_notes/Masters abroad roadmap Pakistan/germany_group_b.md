# Germany Group B: verification notes (target intake WS 2027/28)

Researched: 2026-10-18. Sources are official university pages unless marked otherwise.
Applicant: BS CS (4 yr) FAST-NUCES, CGPA 3.41/4, ~1.5-2 yrs AI/LLM engineer. No IELTS, no GRE. Plan: English MOI letter, Duolingo or PTE as fallback.

**Grade conversion (modified Bavarian formula, my own calculation):** 1 + 3 x (4.0 - 3.41) / (4.0 - 2.0) = **about 1.9 (German)**, assuming a 2.0 minimum pass CGPA. If the university uses 1.0 as the minimum, it comes out at about 1.6. Each university converts grades its own way.

**Status labels:** VERIFIED = confirmed on an official page during this session. UNVERIFIED = from memory or a third-party source, or the official page did not say.

**Deadlines:** None of these universities had published WS 2027/28 dates on the pages checked. All dates below are the WS 2026/27 cycle (or a recurring rule) and are marked as such.

---

## Headline findings

1. **The MOI letter is accepted at only 3 of the 11 programmes:** FAU AI M.Sc., Passau CS, and Passau AI Engineering. LMU also accepts an English-medium letter, but only for its successor programme (see below). FAU Data Science exempts degrees taught in English, but the wording should be confirmed for Pakistani degrees. Saarland, Bonn and TUHH explicitly reject MOI letters.
2. **Duolingo is not accepted** at any programme where the page addressed it. FAU AI says so explicitly. Saarland, Bonn, TUHH and Passau do not list it. **PTE Academic is accepted at Saarland (76) and Bonn (76).** TUHH does not accept PTE.
3. **GRE (or GATE) is mandatory at Saarland (CS and DSAI) and at FAU AI M.Sc.** for non-EU degree holders like this applicant.
4. **The LMU M.Sc. Data Science has been discontinued.** Its last intake was WS 2024/25. The successor is the M.Sc. Statistics and Data Science, which expects a Statistics or Data Science major, minor or focus in the bachelor's degree.
5. **TUHH M.Sc. Computer Science is not suitable for visa applicants.** TUHH itself says it has a late window (1 June to 15 July) and that applicants who need a student visa should choose a different programme.
6. **FAU will charge non-EU tuition from summer semester 2027.** The fee is set per programme, and most master's programmes are EUR 2,000 per semester. A WS 2027/28 entrant would pay it.

## 1. Saarland University: M.Sc. Computer Science and M.Sc. Data Science and AI (DSAI)
- **Language:** English. **Duration:** 4 semesters (UNVERIFIED this session; standard).
- **Tuition / fee:** No tuition fees; semester contribution about EUR 300-370 (UNVERIFIED this session).
- **WS deadline:** **15 May** (recurring rule, VERIFIED). Lectures start mid-October; decisions by mid-July. WS 2027/28 dates not separately published.
- **Route:** Direct, via the CS department's own system at apply.cs.uni-saarland.de. No uni-assist.
- **MOI letter:** **NOT accepted.** The page says: "A confirmation of your university or a MOI (Medium of Instruction) is not a sufficient equivalent."
- **Duolingo / PTE:** Duolingo not listed. **PTE Academic min. 76** accepted. IELTS min. 7.0. Home editions are accepted.
- **GRE:** **REQUIRED** (GRE General or GATE) for applicants without a degree from the EHEA, Switzerland or the UK. No minimum score.
- **Key requirements:** CGPA at least 75% or 3.00/4 **and** top 10% of the graduating class.
- **Competitiveness for 3.41:** Medium-hard. 3.41 clears the GPA floor, but the top-10% rank and the GRE are the real hurdles. The applicant would need a class-rank certificate from FAST.
- **City tech jobs (Saarbrücken):** 2.5/5. Small market, but very strong research employers (DFKI, MPI-INF, MPI-SWS, CISPA).
- **Source:** https://saarland-informatics-campus.de/en/studying/application/

## 2. University of Bonn: M.Sc. Computer Science
- **Language:** English. **Duration:** 4 semesters (UNVERIFIED this session).
- **Tuition / fee:** **Tuition free, and no application fee** (VERIFIED). Semester contribution about EUR 300-330 (UNVERIFIED).
- **WS deadline:** Non-EU applicants without a German bachelor's "must generally apply in the first application period" (VERIFIED). The exact dates are on the department's "deadlines at a glance" page, which I could not fetch. A third-party site (myguide.de) gives 1 May for non-EU applicants (UNVERIFIED).
- **Route:** Direct, via the university portal at application.uni-bonn.de. No uni-assist.
- **MOI letter:** **NOT accepted.** "We do not accept a proof that English was the language of instruction in your Bachelor's."
- **Duolingo / PTE:** Duolingo not listed. **PTE Academic min. 76** accepted. Other accepted proofs: IELTS 7.0, TOEFL 95 (old scale) or 5.0 (new scale), Cambridge CAE or higher. CEFR C1 is required.
- **GRE:** **Not required** (VERIFIED).
- **Key requirements:** 14 ECTS in algorithm theory, complexity and formal languages; 18 ECTS in math foundations; 18 ECTS in programming, software and information systems; a bachelor's thesis of at least 12 ECTS. Only grades better than 2.5 (German) earn selection points. "Not enough credits in algorithm theory" is a common reason for rejection.
- **Competitiveness for 3.41:** Medium. At about 1.9 German, the grade earns points, but admission is points-based and competitive.
- **City tech jobs (Bonn / Cologne region):** 3.5/5. Telekom, DHL, federal IT, and the Cologne startup scene.
- **Sources:** https://www.informatik.uni-bonn.de/en/studies/faqs/faq-admission and https://www.informatik.uni-bonn.de/en/studies/master-programs/master-program-application

## 3. FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg: M.Sc. Artificial Intelligence
- **Language:** English (implied by the English requirement; programme is English-taught). **Duration:** 4 semesters (UNVERIFIED this session).
- **Tuition / fee:** **Non-EU tuition from SS 2027** for new students, so it **applies to a WS 2027/28 entrant**. Most master's programmes are EUR 2,000 per semester, but the AI-specific amount is UNVERIFIED. There is also a semester contribution (about EUR 100-130, UNVERIFIED) and a **EUR 100 non-EU processing fee per application** (VERIFIED).
- **WS deadline:** The normal window is **15 Feb to 31 May**. The WS 2026/27 window was shortened to 15 Apr to 31 May 2026 because of a change to the examination regulations. Mostly winter intake only.
- **Route:** Direct, via the campo.fau.de portal.
- **MOI letter:** **ACCEPTED.** "If your Bachelor's was taught exclusively in English, a university confirmation (MOI) is enough." Note that the programme's FAQ page says MOI is not accepted, but that page appears to cover the bachelor's programme.
- **Duolingo / PTE:** **Duolingo explicitly NOT accepted.** PTE is not mentioned; the standard is TOEFL 80 or "equivalent" (PTE status UNVERIFIED).
- **GRE:** **REQUIRED** for non-EU applicants from countries that have not signed the Lisbon Convention (Pakistan has not). GRE or GATE accepted; the page says scores should be above average (percentile above 60%). Send scores to FAU, code 1344.
- **Key requirements:** "Excellent" bachelor's degree. Minimum ECTS: Programming & Algorithmics 20, Theoretical CS & Logic 15, Databases 5, Math (analysis, linear algebra, stochastics) 20, Software Engineering 10, plus a 12-ECTS thesis. **Mandatory 60-minute online screening test** for everyone.
- **Competitiveness for 3.41:** Hard. "Excellent degree" is required, plus the GRE and the screening test.
- **City tech jobs (Erlangen / Nuremberg):** 3.5/5. Siemens (Healthineers, Energy), Fraunhofer IIS, Schaeffler, DATEV; Munich is about 1.5 hours away.
- **Sources:** https://www.ai.study.fau.eu/prospective-students/master-ai/application-master/ and https://www.fau.eu/studying/international-students/application-and-enrollment-for-international-applicants/tuition-fees-for-students-from-non-eu-states/

## 4. FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg: M.Sc. Data Science
- **Language:** English ("completely in English"). **Duration:** 4 semesters. Intake in winter and summer.
- **Tuition / fee:** Same FAU non-EU tuition from SS 2027 (amount for this programme UNVERIFIED, likely EUR 2,000 per semester), plus the semester contribution and the EUR 100 processing fee.
- **WS deadline:** **31 May** (SS deadline 30 Nov). Recurring rule on the fact sheet.
- **Route:** Direct, via campo.fau.de.
- **MOI letter:** **Likely accepted.** Applicants "whose university entrance qualification or first degree was taught in English" are exempt from English proof. Confirm that an MOI letter satisfies this.
- **Duolingo / PTE:** Not listed on the programme page. The general FAU requirement is B2 (UNVERIFIED for Duolingo and PTE).
- **GRE:** Not mentioned, so it is probably not required.
- **Key requirements:** Bachelor's in Math, CS, Data Science, Physics or similar. **GPA 2.5 or better (German)**. Applicants at 2.6-2.8 get an online interview on calculus, linear algebra, algorithms and data structures. No ECTS breakdown is given on the page.
- **Competitiveness for 3.41:** **Good.** About 1.9 German clears the 2.5 threshold. This is the best fit of the FAU pair.
- **City tech jobs:** 3.5/5 (as above).
- **Source:** https://www.fau.eu/degree-program/data-science-m-sc

## 5. LMU Munich: M.Sc. Data Science (DISCONTINUED)
- **Status:** The M.Sc. Data Science is **discontinued**. The last intake was WS 2024/25, with an application deadline of 1 June 2024 (per the LMU application portal efv-datascience.ifi.lmu.de).
- **Successor programme: M.Sc. Statistics and Data Science (Department of Statistics)**
  - **Language / Duration:** Not stated on the page; English B2 is required (both UNVERIFIED).
  - **Tuition / fee:** LMU charges no tuition, only the student-services fee and semester ticket (about EUR 85 + ticket). This comes from the old Data Science FAQ; the successor's fee is UNVERIFIED.
  - **WS deadline:** Portal opens 1 Apr. **Department deadline 15 May** (strict). **International Office deadline 15 Jul.** Recurring rule.
  - **Route:** Direct department portal, **plus** a separate application to the LMU International Office for non-EU applicants. No uni-assist mentioned.
  - **MOI letter:** **Accepted.** The page asks for "B2 (or English degree)" and accepts "an official letter confirming English was the language of instruction".
  - **Duolingo / PTE:** The page says "all common B2 certificates", with no specific list (UNVERIFIED).
  - **GRE:** Not mentioned.
  - **Key requirements:** **Bachelor's (180 ECTS) in Statistics or Data Science "as a major, minor, or focus"**, with at least 150 ECTS proven by the time of application. Aptitude score out of 20: grade 5, statistical learning and modelling 6, math foundations of statistics 5, statistical software and programming 4. 18-20 means direct admission, 10-17 a second stage, 0-9 rejection.
  - **Competitiveness for 3.41:** **Poor fit.** A BS CS without a statistics focus will probably score low on the statistics categories. It is only viable if the transcript has substantial probability, statistics and ML content.
  - **City tech jobs (Munich):** 5/5. The best tech market in Germany.
- **Sources:** https://www.stat.lmu.de/en/studies/interested-master/ and https://efv-datascience.ifi.lmu.de/

## 6. TU Hamburg (TUHH): M.Sc. Computer Science
- **Language:** English. **Duration:** 4 semesters (UNVERIFIED this session).

- **WARNING:** TUHH says this programme "is not internationally oriented and therefore has later application periods! Applicants who need student visa should apply for a different degree program!"
- **Competitiveness for 3.41:** Unclear on grade (pre-check threshold not published), and **not recommended because of the visa timing**.
- **City tech jobs (Hamburg):** 4.5/5. Airbus, Otto, Google/Meta offices, and a strong startup scene.
- **Source:** https://www.tuhh.de/t3resources/tuhh/download/studium/studieninteressierte/How-to-apply-at-TUHH-alle-engl-MSc-2026.pdf

## Implications for this applicant
- **Usable with an MOI letter, no GRE:** Passau CS, Passau AIE, FAU Data Science (confirm the exemption wording), and LMU Statistics & DS (poor subject fit).
- **Needs a GRE or GATE score:** Saarland CS and DSAI (which also need IELTS 7 or PTE 76), and FAU AI (MOI accepted).
- **Needs a test score; PTE 76 works, Duolingo does not:** Saarland and Bonn.
- **Needs TOEFL or IELTS (no PTE, no Duolingo, no MOI):** TUHH.
- **Duolingo:** Not accepted, or not listed, at any of these 11 programmes. If a test is taken, **PTE Academic (aim for 76 or higher)** is far more useful than Duolingo for this group.
