## 1. Post-study work visas and 2025–2026 rule changes by country

### Takeaway
The main English-speaking destinations have all tightened their rules. The UK Graduate Route drops to 18 months for applications made from 1 Jan 2027. The US moved to a wage-weighted H-1B lottery and added a $100k fee whose legal status is still contested. Canada froze its PGWP field list and cut study permits, but exempts master's students from the cap from 2026. Australia cut the 485 age limit to 35 and raised fees. Continental Europe (Germany, Netherlands, Finland, Sweden) still has clear salary-threshold routes from graduate status to a skilled permit, and some of those thresholds are reduced for recent graduates.

### Cited Findings

**United Kingdom: Graduate Route**
- ENACTED via the Statement of Changes (Oct 2025): Graduate visa applications submitted on or after 1 January 2027 get 18 months instead of 2 years. PhD graduates keep 3 years. — [DavidsonMorris](https://www.davidsonmorris.com/graduate-route-reduced-to-18-months/); [ICEF Monitor](https://monitor.icef.com/2025/10/uk-to-implement-reduced-graduate-route-from-january-2027/); [TLT](https://www.tlt.com/insights-and-events/insight/next-tranche-of-immigration-changes-published)
- The application date decides which length you get, not the course end date. If results are delayed and the university notifies the Home Office after Jan 2027, the applicant may get only 18 months. — [UK Graduate Route: 2 Years or 18 Months?](https://abroadeducounsel.com/blog/uk-visa-rule-changes); [Connaught Law](https://connaughtlaw.com/uk-immigration-white-paper-2025-system-changes/)
- The May 2025 White Paper itself does not change law. The rule change came through the Statement of Changes. Current students on courses longer than 12 months (e.g. two-year master's) are affected. — [House of Commons Library](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10267/); [ICEF Monitor](https://monitor.icef.com/2025/10/uk-to-implement-reduced-graduate-route-from-january-2027/); [UKCISA](https://www.ukcisa.org.uk/news/student-update-changes-to-the-student-and-graduate-rules/)

**United States: OPT / STEM OPT / H-1B**
- ENACTED: DHS final rule (published 29 Dec 2025, effective 27 Feb 2026) replaced the random H-1B lottery with wage-level-weighted selection. A Level IV wage gets about 4x the selection chance of a Level I wage. One source says the FY2027 cap is closed with no second lottery. — [Safeguard Global](https://www.safeguardglobal.com/resources/blog/h1b-visa-changes-2026/); [CTM Legal](https://www.ctmlegalgroup.com/resources/h-1b-cap-season-2026-the-new-lottery-rules-key-deadlines-and-what-changed); [Dewit Law](https://www.dewit.law/what-changed-for-the-h-1b-lottery-in-2026-and-what-employers-need-to-know/). (A BHLG page gives a 2027 effective date, which conflicts with the others and is probably an error: [BHLG](https://bhlg.com/h-1b-lottery-changes/))
- $100,000 H-1B proclamation fee: STATUS CONTESTED. One source says a federal court vacated it in June 2026 and a new proclamation on 18 Sept 2026 extended it. Another says a Massachusetts court ruled it unlawful on 8 June 2026 and then paused its own ruling, so the fee is back in force pending appeal. — [Consultadd](https://consultadd.com/blog/h1-b-every-rule-change-and-where-it-stands-now); [EduConnect USA](https://educonnectusa.com/2026/06/h1b-2026-international-students/)
- The fee generally targets beneficiaries outside the US. Change-of-status petitions for people already in valid status, including F-1 OPT holders, are generally not subject to it. — [Prodigy Finance](https://prodigyfinance.com/resources/blog/h-1b-in-2026-the-100-000-fee-wage-based-lottery-and-what-it-means-for-students/); [EduConnect USA](https://educonnectusa.com/2026/06/h1b-2026-international-students/)
- PROPOSED, NOT CONFIRMED: a separate proposed fee rule (about $103,265 for cap-subject petitions) is mentioned in secondary sources. Its status needs verification with USCIS/DHS. — [Consultadd](https://consultadd.com/blog/h1-b-every-rule-change-and-where-it-stands-now)
- No formal 2026 OPT regulatory change was found. Commentary says the wage-weighted lottery makes converting from OPT to H-1B harder for entry-level (Level I) graduates. — [United OPT](https://www.unitedopt.com/Home/blogdetail/h-1b-lottery-2026-changes-what-the-new-system-for-opt-students)

**Canada: PGWP and study permit caps**
- IRCC froze the PGWP eligible field-of-study list for 2026, with no additions or removals. Field-of-study requirements apply to study permit applications made on or after 1 Nov 2024. University degree graduates (including master's) are not restricted by CIP code. — [CIC News](https://www.cicnews.com/2026/01/year-in-review-what-changed-for-international-students-and-pgwp-holders-in-canada-in-2025-0165602.html); [Erickson Immigration Group](https://eiglaw.com/canada-freezes-pgwp-eligible-field-of-study-list-for-2026/); [immigration.ca](https://immigration.ca/eligible-fields-of-study-for-canadas-pgwp-will-not-change-in-2026/); [amirismail.com](https://www.amirismail.com/canada-pgwp-eligible-programs-2026/)
- From 1 Jan 2026, master's and doctoral students at public institutions are exempt from the study permit cap. — [Career Launcher](https://www.careerlauncher.com/blogs/study-abroad/canada-study-permit-changes-2026/); [CIC News](https://www.cicnews.com/2026/01/year-in-review-what-changed-for-international-students-and-pgwp-holders-in-canada-in-2025-0165602.html)
- The cap figures conflict. One source gives up to 408,000 study permits in 2026 (−7%). Others give 155,000 new arrivals (−49%). These may measure different things. — [Career Launcher](https://www.careerlauncher.com/blogs/study-abroad/canada-study-permit-changes-2026/); [visaflo.ca](https://visaflo.ca/blog/pgwp-eligibility-field-of-study-freeze-2026)
- PGWP language requirement: CLB/NCLC 7 for degree graduates. Living-funds requirement outside Quebec is C$23,448. — [hihalifax](https://hihalifax.com/en/pgwp-eligibility-canada/); [Career Launcher](https://www.careerlauncher.com/blogs/study-abroad/canada-study-permit-changes-2026/)

**Australia: Subclass 485 (Temporary Graduate)**
- The age limit was cut to 35 or under at application (from 50). There are exceptions for HK/BNO passport holders, and some sources mention research master's/PhD. — [ApplyBoard](https://assist.applyboard.com/hc/en-us/articles/6478400793741-Australia-Subclass-485-Visa-Guide-2026-Requirements-Fees); [Edvise Hub](https://www.edvisehub.com/485-visa-changes-2026-age-limit-exceptions-what-you-must-know-now/)
- Duration figures conflict. One source says 2 years for a bachelor's/coursework master's and 3 years for a doctorate. Another gives up to 3 years for a research master's/PhD. A third gives PhD 4 years. — [Questra](https://questra.com.au/blog/485-visa-2026-everything-you-need-to-know/); [EPA Global](https://epaglobal.com/blog-485-visa-2026-changes-explained/); [Premier Visa Group](https://www.premiervisagroup.com/post/australia-485-graduate-visa-guide-1)
- Primary applicant fee is reported at AUD 5,750 from 1 July 2026, after a rise to AUD 4,600 on 1 March 2026. IELTS 6.5 minimum, and English tests are valid for 1 year. — [RACC](https://www.racc.net.au/temporary-graduate-visa-tr485); [vNext Overseas](https://vnextoverseas.com/blog/australia-485-visa-2026-changes)

**Germany: EU Blue Card**
- 2026 standard threshold: €50,700 gross/yr (about €4,225/mo). Reduced threshold: €45,934.20 for shortage occupations (including IT/STEM) and for graduates within 3 years of their degree. At the reduced level the Federal Employment Agency must approve the job. — [Jobbatical](https://www.jobbatical.com/blog/germany-latest-eu-blue-card-salaries-updated); [FindEnglish.de](https://www.findenglish.de/blog/eu-blue-card-germany-2026-requirements-salary-threshold-how-to-apply); [Kagan Legal](https://kaganlegal-germany.com/en/eu-blue-card-germany/)
- IT specialists without a degree but with 3+ years of experience can also qualify at the lower threshold. — [Kompass Global Advisory](https://kompassglobaladvisory.com/insights/germany-eu-blue-card-salary-2026)

**Netherlands: kennismigrant / zoekjaar**
- 2026 kennismigrant gross monthly thresholds (excluding 8% holiday allowance): €5,942 for age 30+, €4,357 for under 30, and €3,122 for recent graduates (zoekjaar-eligible, applying within 3 years of graduating). The salary must also be at market rate. Thresholds are indexed every 1 Jan. — [Jobbatical](https://www.jobbatical.com/blog/netherlands-highly-skilled-migrant-permit-kennismigrant-guide); [dutch-law.com](https://dutch-law.com/employment-law/highly-skilled-migrant-netherlands.html); [findlawyer.nl](https://findlawyer.nl/kennismigrant-visa/)
- The 30% ruling salary threshold is cited at about €48,013/yr in 2026. — [Jobbatical](https://www.jobbatical.com/blog/netherlands-highly-skilled-migrant-permit-kennismigrant-guide). (A study-abroad.org guide gives different, lower thresholds and is treated as unreliable: [study-abroad.org](https://www.study-abroad.org/blog/nl-zoekjaar-guide/))
- The zoekjaar (orientation year) is a 1-year job-search permit for graduates. — [study-abroad.org](https://www.study-abroad.org/blog/nl-zoekjaar-guide/)

**Ireland: Stamp 1G and Critical Skills**
- Critical Skills Employment Permit (CSEP) minimum salary from 1 March 2026: €40,904 for a role on the Critical Skills Occupation List (previously €38,000). Roles off the list need €68,911 (previously €64,000). — [Citizens Information](https://www.citizensinformation.ie/en/moving-country/working-in-ireland/employment-permits/green-card-permits/); [T&S Law](https://tandslaw.com/ireland-updated-annual-salary-thresholds-for-employment-permits/)
- The recent-graduate CSEP rate is reported at about €36,848. — [CareersMines](https://careersmines.com/blog/ireland-critical-skills-permit-2026)
- Stamp 1G is a graduate permission (often described as up to 2 years for level-9 master's graduates) and has no salary threshold. That comes from a weak source. — [EEC Global](https://eecglobal.com/blog/ireland-stay-back-visa-2026); [Cokonet](https://cokonetoverseas.com/blog/ireland-stamp-1g-post-study-work-guide)

**Sweden**
- ENACTED 1 June 2026: the work permit salary floor rose from 80% to 90% of the median wage. With the median at SEK 38,300 from 16 June 2026, the floor is SEK 34,470/month. Some groups use 75%. Pay must also match the collective agreement or customary level, and you cannot combine two jobs to meet it. — [Nordic Life Guide](https://nordiclifeguide.com/en/sweden/work-permit-salary-requirement/); [Zalaris](https://zalaris.com/resources/blog/new-salary-thresholds-for-swedish-work-permits-in-2026-what-changed-on-1-june-and-what-to-do-now); [Jobbatical](https://www.jobbatical.com/blog/sweden-work-permit-salary-threshold-2026)

**Finland**
- 2026 specialist permit: at least €3,937/month plus a higher education degree. The first permit lasts up to 2 years. Fringe benefits do not count toward the salary. — [Migri](https://migri.fi/en/specialist); [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/2026/flash-alert-2026-016.html)
- Post-study job-search permit: up to 2 years after a Finnish degree, with about €800/month of funds needed. This is from a single consultancy source. — [Ardent Overseas](https://ardentoverseas.com/study-in-finland/post-study-work-visa/)

### Inferences
- **US is the highest-variance option.** OPT holders who change status inside the US appear exempt from the $100k fee, but the wage-weighted lottery penalises entry-level (Level I) offers. A fresh master's graduate's odds of getting an H-1B within the OPT + STEM OPT window are now worse than before 2026.
- Background knowledge NOT verified this session: STEM OPT is 12 months OPT + 24-month STEM extension (36 months total), and F-1 students who are nonresident aliens are generally exempt from FICA for their first 5 calendar years. Verify both on uscis.gov / irs.gov.
- **Graduate-friendly thresholds:** Germany €45,934 for recent graduates, the Netherlands €3,122/mo after the zoekjaar, and Ireland's CSEP €36,848–40,904 are all reachable with typical entry-level AI/SWE pay. Sweden's rule is now harder for low-paid first jobs, but SWE pay is usually well above SEK 34,470.
- For a 3–5 year horizon, the UK's 18 months is the tightest. A Skilled Worker sponsorship is needed within that window to stay longer.

### Gaps
- **Italy:** No 2026 source found on the post-study "permesso per attesa occupazione" (commonly described as 12 months) or Italian Blue Card thresholds. Verify on Italian Interior Ministry/Questura sources.
- **Hungary:** No source found on the 2024+ guest-worker regime or a graduate job-search permit. Unverified.
- **Korea (D-10 job-seeker → E-7), Japan (Designated Activities job-hunting → Engineer/Specialist), Malaysia (Employment Pass categories), Türkiye (work permit):** No 2026 policy sources retrieved.
- **Australia:** The 2026 international student cap (National Planning Level) was not found in results.
- The US $100k fee's current enforcement status (Oct 2026) is contested between sources. Check the USCIS H-1B page and court docket before relying on it.
- No official government pages (gov.uk, uscis.gov, canada.ca, homeaffairs.gov.au, ind.nl, irishimmigration.ie, make-it-in-germany.com) were fetched directly. All figures should be re-checked there.

## 2. Job market in 2026 and English-only employability by country

### Takeaway
2026 is a split market. AI/ML engineer roles are growing and pay is rising sharply, while generalist and especially entry-level software roles have shrunk. Layoffs continue at around 133k tech workers through mid-October. For an English-only graduate, the realistic markets are the Anglosphere, the Netherlands, Ireland, Berlin, Stockholm and the Gulf. Japan, Korea, Italy and Türkiye are hard without the local language, except at a small set of international employers.

### Cited Findings
- ML/AI engineer listings are up 59% vs the 2020 baseline. Generalist SWE listings are down 49% from the 2022 peak. — [Pin.com tech job market report](https://www.pin.com/blog/tech-job-market-report/)
- AI engineer salaries rose 20–30% year on year in 2026. — [HeroHunt](https://www.herohunt.ai/blog/tech-job-market-2026-what-the-data-shows/)
- Layoffs.fyi tally (as cited): 133,217 tech employees laid off at 323 companies in 2026 through 15 Oct. Cuts are concentrated in mid-career generalist roles at large employers. — [HeroHunt layoffs](https://www.herohunt.ai/blog/tech-layoffs-and-ai-the-2026-reality-check/); [Acquism](https://acquism.com/articles/what-2026s-tech-layoffs-really-mean-for-engineers/)
- Indeed: only 4.5% of US software development postings in Q1 2026 were entry-level. The scale of the junior decline varies by source (about −40% to −67%). A Stanford HAI figure says entry-level developer employment fell nearly 20% from its 2024 peak. — [Rockstar Developer University](https://rockstardeveloperuniversity.com/entry-level-software-engineer-job-market-statistics/); [Final Round AI](https://www.finalroundai.com/blog/software-engineering-job-market-2026)
- Counter-signals: Robert Half reports 78% of tech leaders plan to increase permanent headcount in H2 2026, and IBM plans to triple entry-level hiring. — [Copilot Interview](https://copilotinterview.com/blog/tech-hiring-statistics-2026); [JobsByCulture](https://jobsbyculture.com/blog/tech-hiring-rebound-summer-2026)
- Japan: Mercari, Rakuten, SmartNews, Indeed Japan, Stripe Japan and many Tokyo startups run engineering teams in English. The Engineer/Specialist visa does not require JLPT. — [Talenlio](https://talenlio.ai/blogs/how-to-get-tech-job-japan-2026); [Japan Dev](https://japan-dev.com/jobs-in-japan-for-english-speakers); [Hello World Japan](https://helloworldjapan.com/en/articles/japan-tech-jobs-foreign-engineers-guide)
- Korea: software development is a common E-7 category. The guide marks Korean as "optional (high in IT)", with typical pay of KRW 3.5M–6.0M+/month. — [90 Day Korean](https://www.90daykorean.com/work-in-korea-for-foreigners/); [dev-korea.com](https://dev-korea.com/jobs)

## 3. Salaries (entry and mid-level AI/ML and SWE)

### Takeaway
Cross-country salary data in 2026 is fragmented and mostly from aggregators that use inconsistent methods. The US pays 2–3x Europe (Levels.fyi medians: US $192.5k, Canada $96k, Europe $74k). Ireland, the Netherlands, Germany and the UK cluster around $65–115k averages. Japan and Korea are about $50–68k, and Malaysia far lower. Gulf AI engineers with 2–5 years of experience earn about AED 20–35k or SAR 20–30k per month, tax-free.

## 4. Savings calculations by country (single person, modelled)

### Takeaway
On the modelled numbers, the **US on OPT (no FICA) and the Gulf** have by far the highest savings potential, at about $40–65k/yr (PKR 11–18M). The next tier is **mid-level roles in Australia, the Netherlands (30% ruling), Germany, Ireland (shared housing) and London**, at about $18–26k/yr. Entry-level savings in most of Europe are only about $5–15k/yr (PKR 1.4–4M). London at entry level, Italy and Türkiye save close to zero. A no-master's Gulf route at 2–3 years of experience (about $16–49k/yr) matches or beats every non-US master's route in years 1–3, without tuition debt.

### Cited Findings
- Cited inputs are limited to the salary ranges, visa thresholds and Gulf pay figures in Sections 1 and 3 (e.g. Riyadh mid-level SAR 20–30k tax-free — [PayMetric Labs](https://www.paymetriclabs.com/sa/insights/ai-engineer-salary-saudi-arabia-2026). Dubai AED 18–45k/month — [JobXDubai](https://jobxdubai.com/salaries-2026/aiml-engineer-data-and-analytics-data-science-ai-salary-dubai-2026), [Angel in Dubai](https://www.angelindubai.com/post/ai-machine-learning-engineer-salary-dubai-uae-2026)). Tax, rent and living costs are not cited (see Gaps).

### Inferences: savings model **[all rows are assumptions; verify with Numbeo and official tax calculators]**

Savings exclude remittances, loan repayments, flights home and pension contributions you cannot withdraw. PKR at 278/USD.

Interpretation:
- **3–5 year cumulative view (illustrative):** Germany or the Netherlands, entry for 2 years then mid for 2 years, comes to about $50–80k total (PKR 14–22M). A US route with OPT then H-1B gives about $250k+ over 4 years if the H-1B is won, but it carries high lottery and job-market risk. The Gulf over 4 years gives about $65–200k (PKR 18–55M), starting immediately with no tuition.
- **Net of master's cost:** subtract tuition plus living costs during study (from the cost research in other notes). For tuition-free or low-tuition Germany/Finland/Italy/Hungary programmes, the payback is faster. For the US/UK/Australia, a high tuition bill can consume 1–3 years of savings.
- **Gulf caveats:** Pakistani nationals may face lower pay offers than Western passport holders. Housing allowances often raise effective savings, and employer-provided housing can push Dubai savings toward AED 200k+. There is no path to permanent residency (except UAE Golden Visa for high earners), which fits a "save and return to Pakistan" goal well.
- **Biggest swing factors:** housing choice (shared vs 1-bed adds €10–13k/yr in Dublin/Amsterdam/London), US FICA exemption on F-1, the Dutch 30% ruling (unverified future reductions), and Italy's impatriate tax regime (not verified; could materially raise Italian net pay).
