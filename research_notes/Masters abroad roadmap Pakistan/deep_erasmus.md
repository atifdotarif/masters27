## 2. DEAI exact procedure (Application Manual V1.0, downloaded and read) [V]
1. Register at https://deai.ulb.ac.be/emundus/ using Chrome or Firefox. You need a first name, surname, email, username and a password of at least 4 characters. A confirmation email with your login details follows.
2. Complete the forms, pressing "Save & Continue" after each one:
   - Personal details. Ranking the specialties is for statistics only. At the end you choose a scholarship category, and the manual says a wrong category "may imply automatic exclusion".
   - Academic details. You give the curriculum URL, and the university must appear in the IAU-based drop-down.
   - References. Give 0-3 referees with name, email, phone, address and webpage, plus an optional H-index taken from Google Scholar.
   - Work experience.
   - Language.
3. Fill in the macro-enabled self-evaluation Excel:
   - You set the minimum and maximum passing marks, or letter-grade equivalences.
   - The sheet converts your marks into 4 intervals (1-4) and computes ECTS as 60 × credits / credits per year.
   - It also computes the totals for ACM major, ACM minor and CS subjects.
   - Unprotecting cells or changing formulas means automatic exclusion.
4. Upload the files. Each must be 2 MB or less and in English. The mandatory set is photo, cover letter, passport, CV, degree, transcripts, Excel and English proof. Work letters and the FYP report are optional but scored.
5. Referees get an automatic email with a 4-5 question form and upload a PDF letter. Letters can arrive after you submit, as long as they meet the reference deadline (18 Jan 2027). The portal shows each letter's status, and you can delete and re-add a referee to trigger a new email.
6. Submit by ticking the declaration and pressing "Submit my form!". Early files are checked and you are told if something needs fixing. To change anything after submission, email deai_admissions@cs.ulb.ac.be; your file is reopened and you must resubmit.
7. Results: no date is published. The last cycle announced on 7 Feb 2026 [U for 2027].

## 3. Visas (first-semester country)
**Belgium (DEAI, ULB)** [V, VFS Pakistan checklist + embassy]
- Applications go only through **VFS Global/Gerry's**. TLScontact is not used in Pakistan.
- Fill in the form on visaonweb.diplomatie.be and bring:
  - 2 signed copies of the form and 3 photos
  - a passport valid for at least 3 months beyond the visa
  - the ORIGINAL admission letter (a "provisional registration certificate" is refused)
  - diplomas and transcripts attested by HEC (no apostille needed for the visa)
  - a police certificate covering 5 years, apostilled
  - a medical certificate from an embassy-accredited doctor, in a sealed envelope
  - financial cover; the scholarship certificate is enough, so Annex 32 is not needed
  - proof of the IBZ fee, if it applies
- Processing takes 2-3 weeks, or 4-6 weeks if the file is referred to Brussels. You can apply up to 6 months before travel and at least 3 weeks before it.

**Spain (EMAI; DEAI semester 2)** [partly V]
- Applications go through BLS Pakistan (pak.blsspainvisa.com, "Study Visa" page). The national visa fee is **PKR 29,578** plus a **PKR 5,519 service fee**, paid in cash [V].
- The detailed checklist PDF ("student more than 180 days") returned a 404. The usual Spanish requirements are below, but they are [U]:
  - health insurance with no co-payment
  - an apostilled police certificate with a sworn translation
  - a medical certificate
  - proof of funds
- DEAI students in semester 2 may be able to use EU intra-mobility for students instead of a new visa. Confirm with the consortium [U].

**France (MAIA semester 1 at UBE, unverified order; CYBERUS)**: Campus France "Etudes en France" procedure, then a VLS-TS visa [U].

**Hungary (IPCVai)**: residence permit for study through Enter Hungary [U].

**Finland (IMLEX, EDISS) and Norway (CYBERSURE)**: online residence permit, then a biometrics appointment [U].

## 4. After selection [mostly U]
1. Accept within the window you are given. Examples: CoDaS 13 days (7 days for reserve offers) [V]; EMAI 3-5 days [V].
2. Sign the EMJM Student Agreement.
3. Ask for the final admission letter and the scholarship certificate for the visa.
4. Expect the EUR 1,400/month allowance only after arrival, enrolment and opening a bank account. EMAI, for example, pays only after arrival [V]. Bring EUR 2-3k to cover the first month.

## 5. Catalogue scan method
- The EACEA catalogue page is JavaScript-rendered and was not parsed.
- The erasmuscatalogue.com WordPress API (/wp-json/wp/v2/posts?search=...) returned about 150 programme posts. Each post's EU project end date was used as a funding signal: a 2027-29 intake needs funding until about Oct 2029 or later.
  - Funded to 2029-2030: IPCVai, IMLEX, EMLDS, SMACCs, CDE, EMINENT.
  - Ending 2026-2028 (2027 EM intake unlikely unless renewed): IFRoS, MIR, SSIs, SECCLO, EMECS, QuanTEEM, NeuroData, CyberMACS, ChEMoinformatics+.
- New EMJMs selected 16 Jul 2026 that are relevant: MAIA, EMILDAI, GeoTech, NavigAIte EU, MICROSCOM, S3AID (structural design) and SMARTLight (6G photonics).
- Not found: no pure bioinformatics, digital-health or quantum EMJM with a confirmed 2027 EM intake.
