# Germany: step-by-step research notes (researched 2026-10-18)

The structured output is in `D:/Atif/Masters/masters-app/data/guide-germany.json`. These notes record what was confirmed, where it came from, and what is still uncertain.

## Confirmed on official pages
- **MOFA apostille**: Since Monday 27 July 2026, apostille requests are not accepted without prior online registration at apostille.mofa.gov.pk. You visit Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Quetta or Gujrat on the date you book. The fee is **Rs 3,000 per personal/educational document** (schedule in force since 08 Jul 2024). TCS, Leopards and Gerry's are authorised couriers. The WhatsApp help desk is +92 307 1339682. The same page also carries a notice that the apostille portal was temporarily suspended because of cybersecurity threats. Source: https://mofa.gov.pk/consular-service-attestation-of-documents (fetched with curl because of an SSL certificate error).
- **New MOFA Digital Attestation Portal**: portal-attestation.mofa.gov.pk was launched around 12 Oct 2026 and sends e-certificates by email. This comes from a secondary source, ProPakistani: https://propakistani.pk/2026/10/16/how-to-apply-for-online-mofa-document-attestation/
- **German missions in Pakistan, student visa page**: The Consular Services Portal (CSP) is active for Karachi, which covers Sindh and Balochistan. Because of a known glitch, some mandatory documents show as optional. Degree certificates, transcripts and proof of tuition payment must still be uploaded from the start. https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/2-study-visa-seite-1676104
- **Study visa leaflet (Islamabad, dated 10/2023)**: The visa fee is **EUR 75, paid in PKR**. Your passport must be valid for at least 1 year plus 3 months. You need travel health insurance valid from your travel date. The leaflet gives the old blocked amount of EUR 11,208, which is now outdated. https://pakistan.diplo.de/resource/blob/2208710/4a44b499964d27c04ecb6e5770fdb824/study-visa---leaflet-data.pdf
- **Older Karachi checklist**: It describes a sealed envelope dropped in a box at the gate, and EUR 10,236. This process is outdated now that the CSP is in use. Its document list still includes a CV, a motivation letter and SSC/HSSC copies. https://pakistan.diplo.de/resource/blob/2290888/dbca85b1c7f634f1a1290ec15a71ac76/checklist---student---language-course-visas-data.pdf
- **uni-assist fees**: EUR 75 for the first programme per semester and EUR 30 for each additional one. You can pay by Visa or Mastercard with 3D Secure. A EUR 10 admin fee applies to withdrawals after the 14-day period. https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-apply/pay-all-fees/handling-fees/ and https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-apply/pay-all-fees/payment-options/
- **uni-assist documents**: The process is digital, with uploads in My assist. Certified copies go **by post only if the university requires certification**, so check each university's entry in the list. Scans of certified copies and photocopies are not accepted where certification applies. English-language originals need no translation. https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-apply/send-track/sending-an-application/ , https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-apply/assemble-your-documents/certified-copies-and-translations/ , https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-apply/plan-your-application/educational-certificates-translations/
- **TK student rate from 1 Jan 2026**: 10.22% (EUR 87.38) + Zusatzbeitrag 2.69% (EUR 23.00) + care insurance 4.2% for age 23+ without children (EUR 35.91) = **about EUR 146.29/month**. https://www.tk.de/en/become-a-member/join-tk/contribution-rate-2034364
- **BAMF, EU Blue Card**: Issued for 4 years, or the contract length plus 3 months. The contract must run at least 6 months. Settlement permit after 27 months, or 21 months with B1 German. In the first 12 months, the authority can object to a job change within 30 days. https://www.bamf.de/EN/Themen/MigrationAufenthalt/ZuwandererDrittstaaten/Migrathek/BlaueKarteEU/blauekarteeu-node.html
- **Munich, job-search permit after studies**: Up to 18 months, starting directly after your studies. You must apply before the student permit expires. https://stadt.muenchen.de/service/en-GB/info/hauptabteilung-ii-buergerangelegenheiten/10400883/

## Secondary sources (verified = false in the JSON)
- **HEC e-Attestation**: Live since 11 May 2026 on eservices.hec.gov.pk. It is paperless with no courier. The fee is Rs 3,000/document, paid through a 1-Link consumer number, and the certificate is downloaded from your account. HEC verifies with NADRA and the university. Sources: https://propakistani.pk/2026/05/13/how-to-apply-for-hec-online-degree-attestation/amp/ , https://www.thenews.pk/story/1414960-hec-launches-online-paperless-degree-attestation-system
- **Blocked account**: EUR 11,904/year (EUR 992/month) for 2026. https://www.expatrio.com/about-germany/blocked-amount-2024 (the German mission's finance page is https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/1995528-1995528)
- **Provider fees**: Expatrio charges EUR 119 setup + EUR 9/month from 7 Jul 2026, plus a EUR 100 buffer. Fintiba charges EUR 159 + EUR 9.90/month (since 3 Nov 2025), plus a EUR 100 buffer. Coracle has been paused since Aug 2025. https://www.yourweg.com/resources/expatrio-vs-fintiba-vs-coracle . Check each provider's own pricing page before paying.
- **SBP studies-abroad limits**: Up to USD 70,000 per student per calendar year (tuition plus living costs) and USD 5,000 in cash for initial expenses. These figures come from bank documents and a 2015 SBP press release, and current circulars were not confirmed. https://www.bop.com.pk/Documents/trade%20services/Studies%20Abroad.pdf , https://www.sbp.org.pk/press/2015/Medical-01-Jul-15.pdf . The name of the "Form R" bank form varies by bank, so ask your bank's FX desk.
- **Blue Card thresholds for 2026**: EUR 50,700 standard and EUR 45,934.20 reduced (shortage occupations and recent graduates). https://www.thelocal.de/20251217/the-salary-you-need-in-germany-in-2026-to-get-an-eu-blue-card , EY tax alert.
- **Visa processing and VFS fee**: Reports are inconsistent, from 4–12 weeks up to many months of waiting. One source reports a VFS service fee of about EUR 38. https://www.visaflow.app/blog/german-study-visa-pakistan-guide-2026 , https://thepakistanconnect.com/germany-student-visa-pakistan-passport/
- **Student work rules**: 140 full days or 280 half days per year, and 20 hours/week during lectures. Whether HiWi jobs are exempt from the day count is disputed. https://msingermany.com/blog/student-work-rules-germany-140-days-2026
- **uni-assist processing time**: 4–6 weeks generally, or about 6–7 weeks for Asia according to a secondary source. The official page URL returned a 404.
