# EMJM: EMAI (Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in Artificial Intelligence)

Researched 2026-10-18. The official pages used were upf.edu/web/emai (home, access-admission, scholarships-fees, faqs, academic-information, cohort-2026-2028, partner-universities). They were fetched directly on the research date.

The EACEA catalogue page loads its content with JavaScript, so I couldn't read EMAI's entry there. There is no separate consortium website; upf.edu/web/emai is the programme site.

## VERDICT FOR ATIF (read first)

1. **The 2027 intake (Cohort 2027-2029) does NOT appear to have Erasmus Mundus scholarships.** [OFFICIAL, inferred]
   - The FAQ says: "the EMAI programme will offer EMS in all its **first four editions**."
   - Cohort 2026-2028 is the **4th** cohort ("EMAI Welcomes its 4th Cohort", news of 07.09.2026).
   - The UPF project record lists the grant as running 2022-2027.
   - The 2027 Application page lists **only self-funded windows**: 15/11/2026 to 05/06/2027 for worldwide self-funded applicants, then 06/06 to 06/07/2027 for EU self-funded only. It lists no EMS or fee-waiver window.
   - So the 2027 intake is self-funded only unless the EU renews the grant. I found no renewal announcement. **[UNVERIFIED that no EMS will be added later]** Before relying on this, email the programme (address is on the UPF EMAI contact page) and ask: "Will Cohort 2027-2029 offer EMS or fee waivers?"
2. **English: an MOI letter is NOT accepted, and neither is Duolingo or PTE.** [OFFICIAL]
   - Accepted tests: TOEFL iBT, IELTS Academic or Cambridge only.
   - The exemption covers only degrees from AU, CA (not Quebec), NZ, IE, UK, US, or the four consortium countries (ES, IT, NL, SI).
   - "Candidates from countries not listed above, must provide an English language certificate. **No exceptions or exemptions will be granted.**"
   - Atif won't take IELTS, so his only route is **TOEFL iBT ≥90 with every section ≥22** (TOEFL Home Edition is not accepted) or Cambridge C1/C2.
3. Self-funded, the programme costs €9,000/yr in tuition (€18k total) plus 2 years of living costs in 3 countries. That is not low-cost by his budget standards.
4. **Bottom line:** EMAI is a poor fit for 2027 unless (a) he will take TOEFL iBT and (b) the consortium confirms that EMS exists for 2027. Keep it on the watch list only.

## Required documents (official list)

1. Passport (copies of all passports if you hold more than one nationality)
2. Bachelor's degree, or a signed statement that you will finish before the programme starts
3. Full transcript of every course, showing the CGPA
4. CV in English, **max 2 pages** (any format; longer CVs are automatically rejected)
5. Motivation letter in English, **600 to 800 words** (longer letters are automatically rejected)
6. **1-minute video** introducing yourself, given as a link inside a Word/PDF file, max 30 MB. Scholarship applicants only; self-funded applicants don't need it.
7. **2 recommendation letters**, preferably academic (self-funded: 1). Referees upload them through a link the portal sends them, so give only their emails.
8. EMAI Student Supplement Questionnaire (current-cycle version only)
9. English certificate (TOEFL iBT, IELTS or Cambridge)
10. Optional: evidence of professional experience (e.g. an employer letter), research (citation and link), and extra training

You can't edit an application after submitting it. Applications go through the UPF portal.

## Selection timeline and visas

- **Stages** [OFFICIAL]: administrative pre-screening, then a check of minimum requirements, then committee ranking, then online interviews for the shortlist, then final selection plus a waiting list. Appeals must be filed within 7 days and are answered within 14 days.
- **Results:** "Candidates for the Cohort 2027-2029 will be informed about their application results on a rolling basis" [OFFICIAL]. In past EMS cycles results came around March to April [UNVERIFIED, typical EMJM pattern; not stated on the official pages].
- **Enrolment:** September. 2026 pattern: welcome event 1 to 2 Sept, classes from 14 Sept [OFFICIAL]. Aggregator gives 25 Sep 2027 as the start date [UNVERIFIED].
- **Visa:** the official pages say nothing about visa timing. Sem 1 is in Spain, so the first visa is a **Spanish student visa** applied for in Pakistan once the UPF admission letter arrives, typically 2 to 3 months before September [UNVERIFIED]. Later semesters need a residence permit or visa for Italy, the Netherlands or Slovenia, usually arranged with help from the host university [UNVERIFIED]. If a self-funded applicant applies near the 5 Jun 2027 deadline, visa time gets tight; apply in Nov to Jan.
